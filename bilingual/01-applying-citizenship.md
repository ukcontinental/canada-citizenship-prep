## Applying for Citizenship / 申請入籍

> When you apply for citizenship, officials will check your status, verify that you are not prohibited from applying, and ensure that you meet the requirements.

當您申請入籍時，政府官員會 **核對您的身分**、**確認您未被限制申請**，並 **確認您符合所有要件**。

> Your application may take several months. Please ensure that the Call Centre always has your correct address while your application is being processed.

您的申請可能需要 **數個月** 才會處理完畢。請確保在審理期間 **客服中心 (Call Centre) 始終擁有您正確的地址**。

> See page 54 for telephone numbers.

電話號碼請見指南第 54 頁。

## How to Use This Booklet to Prepare for the Citizenship Test / 如何使用本書準備公民考試

> This booklet will help you prepare for the citizenship test. You should:
> - Study this guide;
> - Ask a friend or family member to help you practise answering questions about Canada;
> - Call a local school or school board, a college, a community centre or a local organization that provides services to immigrants and ask for information on citizenship classes;
> - Take English or French language classes, which the Government of Canada offers free of charge.

本書將協助您準備公民考試。建議您：

- **研讀本指南**；
- **請朋友或家人協助您練習回答** 關於加拿大的問題；
- **聯繫當地學校 / 教育局 / 學院 / 社區中心 / 移民服務機構**，詢問是否有公民考試輔導課程；
- **參加加拿大政府提供的免費英文或法文課程**（LINC / CLIC）。

## About the Citizenship Test / 關於公民考試

> The citizenship test is usually a written test, but it could be an interview. You will be tested on two basic requirements for citizenship: 1) knowledge of Canada and of the rights and responsibilities of citizenship, and 2) adequate knowledge of English or French. Adult applicants 55 years of age and over do not need to write the citizenship test.

公民考試 **通常為筆試**，但也可能改為面試。考試會檢測 **兩項基本要件**：(1) **對加拿大及公民權利與義務的認識**；(2) **足夠的英文或法文能力**。**55 歲以上的成年申請人不需要參加公民考試**。

> The Citizenship Regulations provide information on how your ability to meet the knowledge of Canada requirement is determined. Information about this requirement can be found on page 64 of the study guide.

《公民法規 (Citizenship Regulations)》規定如何判定您是否符合「對加拿大的認識」要件，詳情請見本指南第 64 頁。

> All the citizenship test questions are based on the subject areas noted in the Citizenship Regulations, and all required information is provided in this study guide.

**所有公民考試題目皆出自《公民法規》所列的主題範圍**，且 **所有必備資訊都包含在本指南中**。

## After the Test / 考試之後

> If you pass the test and meet all the other requirements, you will receive a Notice to Appear to Take the Oath of Citizenship. This document tells you the date, time and place of your citizenship ceremony.

若您通過考試並符合所有其他要件，您將收到 **「出席宣誓通知 (Notice to Appear to Take the Oath of Citizenship)」**。此文件會告知您 **入籍典禮 (citizenship ceremony) 的日期、時間與地點**。

> At the ceremony, you will:
> - Take the Oath of Citizenship;
> - Sign the oath form; and
> - Receive your Canadian Citizenship Certificate.

典禮上您將：

- **宣讀公民誓詞**；
- **簽署誓詞文件**；以及
- **領取加拿大公民身分證書 (Canadian Citizenship Certificate)**。

> If you do not pass the test, you will receive a notification indicating the next steps.

若您未通過考試，您將收到通知，告知後續步驟（通常是 **改安排面試** 或 **延期重考**）。

> You are encouraged to bring your family and friends to celebrate this occasion.

典禮歡迎您 **攜伴家人與朋友** 一同慶祝這一刻。
