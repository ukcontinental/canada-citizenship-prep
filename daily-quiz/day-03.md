## 今日重點

### 1. 名稱由來

- 「Canada」來自 **Iroquoian（易洛魁語）的「kanata」**，意思是 **「村莊 village」**｜The name "Canada" comes from the **Iroquoian word "kanata"**, meaning **"village"**
- 1535 年探險家 **Jacques Cartier** 聽到原住民用這個字指稱聚落｜In 1535 the explorer **Jacques Cartier** heard the word used for a settlement

### 2. 早期探險（必記人名與年份）

| 年份 | 探險家 | 事蹟 |
|---|---|---|
| ~1000 | **Vikings 維京人** | 紐芬蘭 L'Anse aux Meadows 短暫定居 |
| 1497 | **John Cabot**（義裔英國） | 首位繪製加拿大東岸地圖的歐洲人 |
| 1534, 1535, 1541 | **Jacques Cartier**（法國） | 探勘聖羅倫斯灣，宣稱土地屬法王 |
| 1604 | **Pierre de Monts** | 建首個法裔殖民地 Port Royal（今新斯科細亞） |
| 1608 | **Samuel de Champlain** | 創建 **魁北克市 Québec City**，被稱「**新法蘭西之父**」 |

### 3. 新法蘭西 New France（1608–1763）

- 中心是 **Québec City**｜Its centre was **Québec City**
- 主要產業是 **毛皮交易**，特別是河狸皮｜The main industry was the **fur trade**, especially beaver pelts
- 與 **Algonquin、Huron** 原住民結盟｜The French allied with the **Algonquin and Huron** peoples
- 對手是與英國結盟的 **Iroquois 易洛魁聯盟**｜Their rivals were the **Iroquois**, who were allied with the British

### 4. 哈德遜灣公司 Hudson's Bay Company（HBC）

- **1670 年** 由英王查理二世授權成立｜Chartered by **King Charles II in 1670**
- 控制 **Rupert's Land 哈德遜灣集水區** 的毛皮貿易｜It controlled the fur trade across **Rupert's Land**, the Hudson Bay watershed
- **加拿大歷史上最古老的公司**，至今仍在｜**The oldest company in Canada**, and it still exists today

### 5. 七年戰爭 / 英法戰爭

- 1756 到 1763 年的全球戰爭｜A global war fought from 1756 to 1763
- **1759 亞伯拉罕平原戰役 Battle of the Plains of Abraham**｜**The Battle of the Plains of Abraham, 1759**
- 戰場就在 **Québec City** 城外｜It was fought just outside **Québec City**
- 英軍 **General Wolfe** 對法軍 **General Montcalm**｜The British **General Wolfe** faced the French **General Montcalm**
- **兩位將軍都陣亡**｜**Both generals died in the battle**
- **英國獲勝**，新法蘭西落入英國手中｜**Britain won**, and New France passed into British hands
- **1763 年《巴黎條約》Treaty of Paris**：法國正式割讓加拿大給英國｜Under the **Treaty of Paris of 1763** France formally ceded Canada to Britain

### 6. 英國統治後的安撫政策

- **1774 年《魁北克法案》Quebec Act**（極重要）｜**The Quebec Act of 1774** — a key exam topic
- **承認法語**｜It **allowed the French language**
- **承認天主教信仰**｜It **allowed the Catholic religion**
- **承認法國民法，用於私法事務**｜It **kept French civil law** for private matters
- 保障 Quebec 法裔身份不被同化｜It protected the French-Canadian identity from assimilation
- 被視為現代加拿大寬容與多元的根基｜It is seen as the foundation of Canadian tolerance and diversity

### 7. 美國獨立的影響

- 1775 到 1783 年的美國獨立戰爭｜The American Revolution, 1775 to 1783
- **約四萬到五萬名 Loyalists 保皇黨** 從美國北逃加拿大｜**About 40,000 to 50,000 Loyalists** fled north to Canada
- 他們奠定了今天 **New Brunswick 與 Ontario** 的雛形｜They laid the foundations of today's **New Brunswick and Ontario**
- 也帶來了大量英語人口｜They also brought a large English-speaking population

## 答案與解析

1. **Iroquoian 易洛魁語**，意思是 **「村莊 village」**。｜**The Iroquoian language**; it means **"village"**.
2. **維京人 Vikings**，約公元 1000 年在紐芬蘭的 **L'Anse aux Meadows**。｜**The Vikings**, around the year 1000, at **L'Anse aux Meadows** in Newfoundland.
3. **1497 年**。｜**1497.**
4. **Samuel de Champlain**。｜**Samuel de Champlain.**
5. 他建立魁北克市，讓法裔殖民地長期生存下來，奠定新法蘭西的基礎。｜He founded Québec City and made the French colony permanent, laying the foundation of New France.
6. **毛皮交易**，特別是河狸皮。｜**The fur trade**, especially beaver pelts.
7. **Algonquin 與 Huron**。｜**The Algonquin and the Huron.**
8. **1670 年**，由 **英王查理二世 Charles II** 授權。｜**1670**, chartered by **King Charles II.**
9. **亞伯拉罕平原戰役 Battle of the Plains of Abraham**。｜**The Battle of the Plains of Abraham.**
10. 英軍 **General Wolfe**、法軍 **General Montcalm**；**兩人皆陣亡，英國獲勝**。｜**General Wolfe** for Britain and **General Montcalm** for France; **both died and Britain won.**
11. **1763 年**。｜**1763.**
12. **法語、天主教信仰、法國民法**。｜**The French language, the Catholic religion and French civil law.**
13. 它讓 Quebec 法裔保住語言、宗教與民法傳統，被視為加拿大寬容與多元的奠基石。｜It let French Canadians keep their language, religion and civil law, and is seen as the cornerstone of Canadian tolerance.
14. **Loyalists**，也稱 **United Empire Loyalists**。｜**The Loyalists**, also called **United Empire Loyalists.**
15. 約 **四萬到五萬人**。｜**About 40,000 to 50,000.**
16. **New Brunswick** 與 **Ontario**。｜**New Brunswick and Ontario.**
17. **John Cabot**，義大利名 Giovanni Caboto。｜**John Cabot**, born Giovanni Caboto.
18. **英國**。｜**Britain.**
19. **哈德遜灣公司 Hudson's Bay Company**。｜**The Hudson's Bay Company.**
20. **1608 年，由 Samuel de Champlain 建立**。｜**In 1608, by Samuel de Champlain.**
