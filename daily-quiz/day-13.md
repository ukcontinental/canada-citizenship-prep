## 今日重點

### 1. 加拿大基本地理

- 加拿大是**世界第二大國家**，僅次於俄羅斯｜Canada is **the second-largest country in the world**, after Russia
- 三大洋環繞：東邊大西洋、北邊北冰洋、西邊太平洋｜Three oceans border it: the Atlantic to the east, the Arctic to the north, the Pacific to the west
- 與美國共享**世界最長的陸地邊界**｜It shares **the longest land border in the world** with the United States
- **加拿大首都是 Ottawa**，位於 Ontario 省｜**The capital of Canada is Ottawa**, in the province of Ontario

### 5. 各省與領地的小知識

- **Quebec** 法裔最多；蒙特婁是世界第二大法語城市，僅次於巴黎｜**Quebec** has the most francophones; Montreal is the second-largest French-speaking city after Paris
- **Ontario** 人口最多、經濟最大；尼加拉瀑布在此｜**Ontario** has the largest population and economy; Niagara Falls is here
- **PEI** 是最小的省，《清秀佳人》的故鄉，靠聯邦大橋連上本土｜**PEI** is the smallest province, home of Anne of Green Gables, linked by the Confederation Bridge
- **Nova Scotia** 的 Halifax 是大西洋區最大港，1917 年曾發生大爆炸｜**Nova Scotia's** Halifax is the region's biggest port, site of the 1917 explosion
- **New Brunswick** 是唯一的官方雙語省｜**New Brunswick** is the only officially bilingual province
- **Manitoba** 是 Métis 文化中心；Winnipeg 有「西部門戶」之稱｜**Manitoba** is the heart of Métis culture; Winnipeg is the Gateway to the West
- **Saskatchewan** 是小麥之省；Regina 有皇家騎警的訓練學院｜**Saskatchewan** is the wheat province; Regina has the RCMP training academy
- **Alberta** 是石油重鎮，境內有洛磯山脈的 Banff 與 Jasper 國家公園｜**Alberta** is oil country, with Banff and Jasper national parks in the Rockies
- **BC** 是太平洋門戶；Vancouver 主辦 2010 年冬季奧運｜**BC** is the Pacific gateway; Vancouver hosted the 2010 Winter Olympics
- **Yukon** 有 1896 年的克朗代克淘金潮，以及加拿大最高峰 Mount Logan｜**Yukon** had the Klondike Gold Rush of 1896 and Canada's highest mountain, Mount Logan
- **NWT** 以天然氣、礦業和極光聞名｜**The Northwest Territories** are known for gas, mining and the northern lights
- **Nunavut** 以因紐特人為主，面積最大而人口最少｜**Nunavut** is mostly Inuit, the largest territory with the smallest population

### 7. 加拿大其他常考地理

- 最高峰是 Yukon 的 **Mount Logan**｜The highest mountain is **Mount Logan** in Yukon
- 最大島是 Nunavut 的 **Baffin Island**｜The largest island is **Baffin Island** in Nunavut
- **加拿大盾 Canadian Shield** 是覆蓋大半國土的古老岩盤｜The **Canadian Shield** is the ancient rock that covers much of the country
- 五大湖中有四個與美國共享，只有 **Lake Michigan 完全在美國境內**｜Four of the Great Lakes are shared with the United States; only **Lake Michigan lies entirely in the U.S.**
