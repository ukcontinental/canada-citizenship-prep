## 今日重點

### 1. 加拿大太平洋鐵路 Canadian Pacific Railway (CPR)

- **1885 年完工**，把東岸和 **British Columbia** 連起來｜**Completed in 1885**, it linked the east to **British Columbia**
- 修這條鐵路是 BC 加入邦聯的條件｜Building it was the condition on which BC joined Confederation
- **約一萬五千名華工**參與興建，許多人死於危險的工程｜**About 15,000 Chinese workers** built it, and many died doing dangerous work
- 鐵路完工後，政府對華人移民課徵 **人頭稅 Head Tax**｜Once the railway was done, the government imposed a **head tax** on Chinese immigrants
- **2006 年**總理 Stephen Harper 代表政府正式道歉｜**In 2006** Prime Minister Stephen Harper apologized on behalf of the government

### 2. Métis 抵抗與 Louis Riel

- **Louis Riel** 是 Métis 的領袖｜**Louis Riel** was the leader of the Métis
- 他領導 **1869 年紅河抵抗**，促成 Manitoba 在 1870 年加入邦聯｜He led the **Red River Resistance of 1869**, which brought Manitoba into Confederation in 1870
- **1885 年西北叛亂** 戰敗，他被處以絞刑｜He was defeated in the **North-West Rebellion of 1885** and hanged
- 他至今仍是加拿大歷史上既受爭議又被紀念的人物｜He remains both a controversial and a celebrated figure in Canadian history

### 3. 婦女投票權（必背順序）

| 年份 | 事件 |
|---|---|
| 1916 | **Manitoba** 首先給予婦女省級投票權（Nellie McClung 等推動） |
| 1917 | 加拿大軍人的妻女取得聯邦投票權 |
| **1918** | **多數加拿大婦女取得聯邦投票權** |
| 1921 | **Agnes Macphail** 成為史上首位女性國會議員 MP |
| 1940 | **Quebec** 婦女取得省級投票權（最晚） |
| **1960** | **原住民** 不必放棄條約身分即可投票 |

「**The Famous Five**」是五位婦女運動者，1929 年的「**Persons Case**」讓婦女在法律上被視為「人」，可被任命進入參議院。｜**The Famous Five** were five women whose 1929 **Persons Case** established that women are "persons" in law and may be appointed to the Senate.

### 4. WWI 第一次世界大戰 1914–1918

- **超過六十萬名加拿大人服役**｜**More than 600,000 Canadians served**
- **約六萬人陣亡**｜**About 60,000 died**
- **1917 年維米嶺戰役 Vimy Ridge**：加拿大軍團首次獨立作戰並獲勝，被視為**民族認同的誕生**｜At **Vimy Ridge in 1917** the Canadian Corps fought as one and won — often called **the birth of the nation**
- **Sir Arthur Currie** 是加拿大史上最傑出的軍人，指揮維米嶺｜**Sir Arthur Currie**, Canada's greatest soldier, commanded at Vimy
- 軍醫 **John McCrae** 寫下名詩 **《In Flanders Fields》**，十一月的罌粟花由此而來｜The military doctor **John McCrae** wrote **"In Flanders Fields"**, the source of the November poppy

### 5. 戰間期（1920–30 年代）

- 1929 年開始的**大蕭條 Great Depression**｜The **Great Depression** began in 1929
- **加拿大廣播公司 CBC 於 1936 年成立**｜The **Canadian Broadcasting Corporation was founded in 1936**

### 6. WWII 第二次世界大戰 1939–1945

- **加拿大在 1939 年 9 月自行對德宣戰**，比英國晚一週，象徵外交獨立｜**Canada declared war on Germany in September 1939**, a week after Britain, as an independent country
- **超過一百萬人服役**｜**More than a million Canadians served**
- **約四萬四千人陣亡**｜**About 44,000 died**
- **1942 年迪耶普戰役 Dieppe**，加軍傷亡慘重｜At **Dieppe in 1942** Canadian losses were heavy
- **1943 年的義大利戰役**｜**The Italian campaign of 1943**
- **1944 年 6 月 6 日 D-Day 諾曼第登陸**，加軍負責 **Juno Beach**｜On **D-Day, June 6, 1944**, Canadians landed at **Juno Beach**
- **1944 至 45 年解放荷蘭**，荷蘭至今每年送鬱金香給加拿大｜Canadians **liberated the Netherlands in 1944 and 1945**; the Dutch still send tulips every year
- 戰時 **日裔加拿大人** 被強制遷離 BC 沿岸並沒收財產｜During the war **Japanese Canadians** were moved away from the BC coast and their property was seized
- **1988 年**總理 Brian Mulroney 道歉並賠償｜**In 1988** Prime Minister Brian Mulroney apologized and compensated them

### 7. 戰時婦女與工業

- 戰時大量婦女進入工廠與軍隊｜Large numbers of women went into the factories and the forces during the war
- 這段經驗改變了戰後婦女在職場的地位｜That experience changed women's place in the workforce after the war

## 答案與解析

1. **1885 年**。｜**1885.**
2. 修鐵路是 BC 在 **1871 年加入邦聯的條件**，鐵路把 BC 與東部連起來。｜It was **the condition of BC joining Confederation in 1871**, and it tied the province to the east.
3. 約 **一萬五千人**。｜**About 15,000.**
4. 針對 **華人移民**；**2006 年**由總理 **Stephen Harper** 道歉。｜**Chinese immigrants**; the apology came in **2006** from Prime Minister **Stephen Harper.**
5. **1869 年紅河抵抗** 與 **1885 年西北叛亂**；戰敗後被**處以絞刑**。｜**The Red River Resistance of 1869 and the North-West Rebellion of 1885**; he was defeated and **hanged.**
6. **Manitoba**，**1916 年**。｜**Manitoba, in 1916.**
7. **1918 年**。｜**1918.**
8. **Agnes Macphail**，**1921 年**。｜**Agnes Macphail, in 1921.**
9. **1940 年**，是最晚的一省。｜**1940** — the last province to do so.
10. **1960 年**。｜**1960.**
11. **1929 年的 Persons Case**，確立婦女在法律上是「人」，可進入參議院。｜**The Persons Case of 1929**, which established that women are "persons" and may sit in the Senate.
12. 服役 **超過六十萬人**，陣亡 **約六萬人**。｜**More than 600,000 served and about 60,000 died.**
13. **維米嶺戰役 Vimy Ridge**。｜**The Battle of Vimy Ridge.**
14. **John McCrae**。｜**John McCrae.**
15. **1939 年 9 月**，比英國晚一週，代表加拿大自主宣戰。｜**In September 1939**, a week after Britain, as an independent decision.
16. 約 **四萬四千人**。｜**About 44,000.**
17. **Juno Beach**。｜**Juno Beach.**
18. **解放荷蘭**，1944 至 1945 年。｜**The liberation of the Netherlands**, 1944 to 1945.
19. **日裔加拿大人**；**1988 年**由總理 **Brian Mulroney** 道歉。｜**Japanese Canadians**; Prime Minister **Brian Mulroney** apologized in **1988.**
