## 今日重點

### 1. 1812 年戰爭 War of 1812（必背！）

- **加拿大對美國**｜**Canada against the United States**
- 美國想併吞英屬北美，入侵失敗｜The United States tried to conquer British North America and failed
- **Major-General Sir Isaac Brock** 在 Queenston Heights 陣亡，被譽為「上加拿大的救星」｜**Major-General Sir Isaac Brock** was killed at Queenston Heights and is remembered as **the Saviour of Upper Canada**
- **Chief Tecumseh** 是原住民領袖，與英軍同盟對抗美國｜**Chief Tecumseh**, a Shawnee leader, allied with the British against the Americans
- **Charles de Salaberry** 在魁北克擊退美軍｜**Charles de Salaberry** turned back an American attack in Quebec
- **Laura Secord** 步行 30 公里向英軍報信，是加拿大歷史最著名的女英雄｜**Laura Secord** walked 30 kilometres to warn the British, and is Canada's best-known heroine
- **1813 年美軍焚毀 York，也就是今天的 Toronto**｜**In 1813 American forces burned York, today's Toronto**
- 報復之下，**1814 年英軍焚燒了華盛頓的白宮**｜In return, **in 1814 British troops burned the White House in Washington**
- 結果加拿大保住領土，**今天的加美邊界由此確立**｜Canada held its territory, and **today's Canada-United States border dates from this war**

### 2. 廢除奴隸制度

- **1793 年** 上加拿大由 **Lieutenant Governor John Graves Simcoe** 率先限制奴隸制｜**In 1793 Lieutenant Governor John Graves Simcoe** made Upper Canada the first province to move against slavery
- **1834 年** 大英帝國全面廢除奴隸制度，加拿大也在其中｜**In 1834** slavery was abolished throughout the British Empire, Canada included
- **地下鐵路 Underground Railroad** 協助美國黑奴逃到加拿大獲得自由｜The **Underground Railroad** helped enslaved people escape from the United States to freedom in Canada

### 3. 1837–38 叛亂 The Rebellions

- 在 **上加拿大（今 Ontario）** 與 **下加拿大（今 Quebec）** 同時發生｜They broke out in both **Upper Canada, today's Ontario**, and **Lower Canada, today's Quebec**
- 領導人是 **William Lyon Mackenzie** 與 **Louis-Joseph Papineau**｜The leaders were **William Lyon Mackenzie** and **Louis-Joseph Papineau**
- 他們要求 **責任政府**，也就是內閣要對民選議會負責｜They demanded **responsible government**, meaning a cabinet answerable to the elected assembly
- 叛亂失敗，卻促成了改革｜The rebellions failed, but they led to reform

### 4. 責任政府的誕生

- **1840 年《統一法案》Act of Union** 把上、下加拿大合併為「加拿大省」｜The **Act of Union of 1840** joined Upper and Lower Canada into the Province of Canada
- **1848 至 1849 年**，Nova Scotia 與加拿大省出現加拿大第一個責任政府｜**In 1848 and 1849** Nova Scotia and the Province of Canada won the first responsible government in Canada
- 關鍵人物是 **Robert Baldwin** 與 **Louis-Hippolyte LaFontaine** 這對英法搭檔｜The key figures were **Robert Baldwin** and **Louis-Hippolyte LaFontaine**, an English-French partnership
- **Sir Louis-Hippolyte LaFontaine** 是第一位由民選多數產生的政府首長｜**Sir Louis-Hippolyte LaFontaine** became the first head of a government chosen by an elected majority

### 5. 邦聯 Confederation 1867（最重要日期）

- **1867 年 7 月 1 日**《**英屬北美法案 British North America Act**》生效，加拿大誕生｜**On July 1, 1867** the **British North America Act** took effect and Canada was born
- 四個建國省份依序是 **Ontario、Quebec、Nova Scotia、New Brunswick**｜The four founding provinces were **Ontario, Quebec, Nova Scotia and New Brunswick**
- 首任總理是 **Sir John A. Macdonald**，$10 鈔票上的人｜The first prime minister was **Sir John A. Macdonald**, who appears on the ten-dollar bill
- 他生於蘇格蘭，被稱為 **加拿大國父**｜Born in Scotland, he is known as a **Father of Confederation**
- 國號是 **Dominion of Canada 加拿大自治領**｜The country was called the **Dominion of Canada**
- 名字取自詩篇 72 篇 8 節，也成了國徽箴言 **A Mari Usque Ad Mare，從海到海**｜The name comes from Psalm 72:8 and gave Canada its motto, **A Mari Usque Ad Mare — from sea to sea**

### 7. 重要早期領袖

- **Sir George-Étienne Cartier**：法裔的邦聯之父，Macdonald 的搭檔｜**Sir George-Étienne Cartier**, the French-Canadian Father of Confederation and Macdonald's partner
- **Sir Étienne-Paschal Taché**：早期的領袖之一｜**Sir Étienne-Paschal Taché**, another early leader
- **Sir Leonard Tilley**（New Brunswick）：推動邦聯｜**Sir Leonard Tilley** of New Brunswick, who pushed for Confederation
- **Sir Charles Tupper**（Nova Scotia）：推動邦聯｜**Sir Charles Tupper** of Nova Scotia, who did the same
- **Sir Wilfrid Laurier**（1896–1911）：**首位法裔總理**，$5 鈔票上的人｜**Sir Wilfrid Laurier**, the **first French-Canadian prime minister**, on the five-dollar bill

## 答案與解析

1. **美國**。｜**The United States.**
2. **Major-General Sir Isaac Brock**。｜**Major-General Sir Isaac Brock.**
3. 站在 **英國與加拿大這一邊**；他是 **Shawnee 原住民首領**。｜He fought **on the British and Canadian side**; he was a **Shawnee chief.**
4. 她**步行約 30 公里，向英軍的 Lieutenant FitzGibbon 通報美軍動向**，讓英軍得以設伏取勝。｜She **walked about 30 kilometres to warn Lieutenant FitzGibbon of an American attack**, allowing the British to win the ambush.
5. **York**，今天的 **Toronto**。｜**York**, today's **Toronto.**
6. **華盛頓的白宮 White House** 以及其他政府建築。｜**The White House** in Washington, along with other government buildings.
7. **1834 年**。｜**1834.**
8. **協助美國黑奴秘密逃往加拿大獲得自由的網絡**，並不是真的鐵路。｜**A secret network that helped enslaved people escape to freedom in Canada** — not an actual railway.
9. **William Lyon Mackenzie** 與 **Louis-Joseph Papineau**。｜**William Lyon Mackenzie and Louis-Joseph Papineau.**
10. 指 **內閣必須對民選議會負責**，而不是只對總督負責。｜It means **the cabinet must answer to the elected assembly**, not only to the governor.
11. **1848 到 1849 年**：1848 是 Nova Scotia，1849 是加拿大省。｜**1848 and 1849** — Nova Scotia in 1848, the Province of Canada in 1849.
12. **1867 年 7 月 1 日**。｜**July 1, 1867.**
13. **Ontario、Quebec、Nova Scotia、New Brunswick**。｜**Ontario, Quebec, Nova Scotia and New Brunswick.**
14. **Sir John A. Macdonald**。｜**Sir John A. Macdonald.**
15. 來自**聖經詩篇 72 篇 8 節**「He shall have dominion also from sea to sea」。｜From **Psalm 72:8**, "He shall have dominion also from sea to sea."
16. **從海到海**。｜**From sea to sea.**
17. **1870 年**。｜**1870.**
18. **1871 年**。｜**1871.**
19. **1949 年**。｜**1949.**
20. **Sir Wilfrid Laurier**，1896 到 1911 年任總理。｜**Sir Wilfrid Laurier**, prime minister from 1896 to 1911.
