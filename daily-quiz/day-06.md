## 今日重點

### 1. 戰後重建與全球角色

- **1945 年加拿大是聯合國 UN 的創始國**｜**Canada was a founding member of the United Nations in 1945**
- **1949 年加拿大是北約 NATO 的創始國**｜**Canada was a founding member of NATO in 1949**
- **1949 年紐芬蘭加入加拿大**，成為第十個省｜**Newfoundland joined Canada in 1949** as the tenth province
- **韓戰 1950 至 1953 年**，加拿大派出超過兩萬六千名軍人｜In the **Korean War of 1950 to 1953** Canada sent more than 26,000 troops
- 加拿大在國際上扮演 **中型大國 middle power** 的角色，長於調停與維和｜Canada acts as a **middle power**, known for mediation and peacekeeping

### 2. 維和 Peacekeeping

- **Lester B. Pearson** 先是外交部長，後來成為總理｜**Lester B. Pearson** was foreign minister and later prime minister
- **1956 年蘇伊士運河危機**，他提議由聯合國派出 **維和部隊** 隔開衝突雙方｜In the **Suez Crisis of 1956** he proposed a **United Nations peacekeeping force** to separate the sides
- **1957 年他因此獲得諾貝爾和平獎**｜**He won the Nobel Peace Prize for it in 1957**
- 加拿大長期是聯合國維和的重要派兵國｜Canada has long been a major contributor to UN peacekeeping

### 3. 1965 國旗

- **1965 年**加拿大採用今天的 **紅白楓葉旗**｜**In 1965** Canada adopted today's **red and white maple leaf flag**
- 在那之前用的是帶英國米字的 **Red Ensign**｜Before that it flew the **Red Ensign**, which carried the Union Jack

### 4. 1969 官方語言法

- 由總理 **Pierre Elliott Trudeau** 推動｜Brought in by Prime Minister **Pierre Elliott Trudeau**
- **英文與法文在聯邦層級地位平等**｜**English and French have equal status at the federal level**

### 5. 1971 多元文化主義

- **Pierre Elliott Trudeau** 宣布多元文化成為官方政策｜**Pierre Elliott Trudeau** made multiculturalism official government policy

### 6. 1982 憲法歸國 + 憲章（最重要）

- **1982 年加拿大完成憲法歸國 Patriation of the Constitution**｜**In 1982 Canada patriated its Constitution**
- 由 **女王伊莉莎白二世** 簽署｜It was signed by **Queen Elizabeth II**
- 其中包含 **《加拿大權利與自由憲章》**｜It contains the **Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms**
- 從此加拿大可自行修憲，不必再經英國國會｜From then on Canada could amend its own Constitution without the British Parliament

### 7. 1995 Quebec 第二次獨立公投

- 結果是留在加拿大 **50.6% 對 49.4%**，僅差 1.2 個百分點｜The result was **50.6% to 49.4%** to stay — a margin of just 1.2 points

### 9. 主要當代政府道歉年份

- **1988 年**為日裔加拿大人道歉，總理 Brian Mulroney｜**1988**, to Japanese Canadians, by Brian Mulroney

- 1989 年加美自由貿易協定、1994 年 NAFTA、**2020 年 CUSMA / USMCA**｜The Canada-US free trade agreement of 1989, then NAFTA in 1994, then **CUSMA in 2020**

5. **1971 年**。｜**1971.**

7. **《加拿大權利與自由憲章》**。｜**The Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms.**

10. **Alexander Graham Bell**。｜**Alexander Graham Bell.**
11. **Joseph-Armand Bombardier**。｜**Joseph-Armand Bombardier.**
12. **James Naismith**，**1891 年**。｜**James Naismith, in 1891.**
13. **Sir Sandford Fleming**。｜**Sir Sandford Fleming.**
14. **Mike Lazaridis 與 Jim Balsillie**，公司原名 RIM。｜**Mike Lazaridis and Jim Balsillie**, whose company was called RIM.
15. **Terry Fox**。｜**Terry Fox.**
16. **2013 年**。｜**2013.**
17. **1988 年**，總理 **Brian Mulroney**。｜**1988**, by Prime Minister **Brian Mulroney.**
18. **2006 年**，總理 Stephen Harper。｜**2006**, by Stephen Harper.
