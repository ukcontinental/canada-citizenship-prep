7. 1812 戰爭中被稱為「Saviour of Upper Canada」的將領是誰？｜Who was called the Saviour of Upper Canada?
8. 1917 年哪一場戰役被視為加拿大民族認同的誕生？｜Which 1917 battle is seen as the birth of the nation?
9. 現行國旗哪一年啟用？｜In what year was the current flag first raised?
10. 國徽箴言是什麼意思？｜What does the national motto mean?
11. 加拿大國會由哪三個部分組成？｜What are the three parts of Parliament?
12. 眾議院總共多少席？｜How many seats are in the House of Commons?
13. 五大主要聯邦政黨，任寫四個。｜Name four of the five main federal parties.
14. 最高法院共有多少位法官？｜How many judges sit on the Supreme Court?
15. 六大公民責任，任寫三條。｜Name three responsibilities of citizenship.
16. 加拿大最大的貿易夥伴是哪一國？｜Who is Canada's largest trading partner?
17. 加拿大屬於 G7 還是 G20？｜Is Canada in the G7 or the G20?
18. 11 月 11 日是什麼節日？｜What day is November 11?
19. **Ontario 現任省長是誰？哪一黨？**｜**Who is the Premier of Ontario, and from which party?**
20. **Ontario 的首府是哪個城市？省議員的縮寫是什麼？**｜**What is Ontario's capital, and what are its legislators called?**

# 第二段：綜合大考（40 題）

### Part A — 公民、權利、原住民、語言（8 題）

21. 憲章保障的四大基本自由是什麼？｜What are the four fundamental freedoms?
22. 加拿大兩種官方語言是什麼？官方語言法哪一年通過？｜What are the two official languages, and when was the Official Languages Act passed?
23. 多元文化主義哪一年成為官方政策？哪位總理推動？｜When did multiculturalism become official policy, and under whom?
24. 「Habeas Corpus」是什麼權利？｜What right is habeas corpus?
25. 2008 年政府對哪一群體道歉？｜Whom did the government apologize to in 2008?
26. 1988 年政府對哪一群體道歉？｜Whom did the government apologize to in 1988?
27. 何時對華工人頭稅道歉？哪位總理？｜When was the head-tax apology, and by which prime minister?
28. 配偶虐待與強迫婚姻在加拿大屬於民事還是刑事？｜Are spousal abuse and forced marriage civil or criminal?

### Part B — 歷史（8 題）

29. 「Canada」一詞來自哪個原住民語言？意思是什麼？｜What language does "Canada" come from, and what does it mean?
30. 1670 年由英王查理二世授權成立的貿易公司是哪一家？｜Which trading company did Charles II charter in 1670?
31. 1759 年亞伯拉罕平原戰役的兩位將軍是誰？結果如何？｜Who were the two generals in 1759, and what happened?
32. 1774 年《Quebec Act》保留法裔哪三項權利？｜Which three rights did the Quebec Act keep?
33. 加拿大邦聯是哪一天？建國四省是哪四個？｜What date was Confederation, and which four provinces?
34. 加拿大首位法裔總理是誰？｜Who was the first French-Canadian prime minister?
35. 太平洋鐵路哪一年完工？對 BC 有什麼意義？｜When was the CPR finished, and what did it mean for BC?
36. 1949 年加入成為第十省的是哪一省？｜Which province joined as the tenth in 1949?

### Part C — 婦女與原住民投票權（4 題）

37. 第一個給婦女省級投票權的省份與年份？｜Which province gave women the provincial vote first, and when?
38. 多數加拿大婦女取得聯邦投票權是哪一年？｜When did most Canadian women get the federal vote?
39. Quebec 婦女取得省級投票權是哪一年？｜When did women in Quebec get the provincial vote?
40. 原住民不必放棄條約身分即可投票是哪一年？｜When could Aboriginal people vote without losing treaty status?

### Part D — 政府、選舉、司法（10 題）

41. 現任總督是誰？她的歷史意義？｜Who is the Governor General, and why is it historic?
42. 參議員如何產生？任職到幾歲？｜How are senators chosen, and until what age?
43. 立法流程的最後一步叫什麼？由誰簽署？｜What is the final step of lawmaking, and who signs?
44. 多數政府與少數政府差別在哪？｜What is the difference between majority and minority government?
45. 哪一個政黨只在 Quebec 競選？｜Which party runs only in Quebec?
46. 「Official Opposition」是什麼？主要職責是什麼？｜What is the Official Opposition, and what does it do?
47. 加拿大三層警力是什麼？｜What are the three levels of policing?
48. 最高法院有幾位法官？由誰任命？｜How many Supreme Court judges are there, and who appoints them?
49. 重大刑案的陪審團人數是多少？｜How many jurors hear a serious criminal case?
50. 司法系統的核心原則，任寫三個。｜Name three core principles of the justice system.

### Part E — 象徵、節日、貨幣、經濟、地理（10 題）

51. 加拿大兩個官方國民運動是什麼？｜What are the two national sports?
52. 國歌哪一年成為官方國歌？皇室國歌是什麼？｜When did O Canada become official, and what is the royal anthem?
53. 真相與和解日是哪一天？｜What date is Truth and Reconciliation Day?
54. 十元鈔票上的人物是誰？她的歷史意義？｜Who is on the ten-dollar bill, and why?
55. Loonie 是多少錢？上面是什麼動物？｜What is the loonie worth, and what is on it?
56. 三大產業類別是什麼？服務業佔就業比例多少？｜What are the three types of industry, and what share of jobs is services?
57. 2020 年取代 NAFTA 的協定簡稱是什麼？｜What replaced NAFTA in 2020?
58. 加拿大五大地理區是哪五個？｜What are Canada's five regions?
59. 加拿大首都是哪個城市？位於哪一省？｜What is Canada's capital, and in which province?
60. Ontario、Quebec、Saskatchewan、Alberta 的首府分別是哪裡？｜Name the capitals of Ontario, Quebec, Saskatchewan and Alberta.
