# Day 12：加拿大經濟 Canada's Economy

## 今日重點

### 1. 加拿大是「貿易大國 Trading Nation」

- **貿易佔 GDP 六成以上**｜**Trade accounts for more than 60% of GDP**
- 加拿大是 **G7 與 G20 的成員**｜Canada belongs to **both the G7 and the G20**
- 加拿大是 **世界上最富裕的國家之一**｜Canada is **one of the wealthiest countries in the world**

### 2. 三大產業類別（常考）

| 類別 | 英文 | 範例 |
|---|---|---|
| 服務業 | **Service Industries** | 交通、教育、健保、銀行、IT、政府服務 |
| 製造業 | **Manufacturing Industries** | 汽車、紙、食品、技術產品 |
| 自然資源業 | **Natural Resources Industries** | 林業、漁業、農業、能源、礦業 |

- **服務業雇用約七成五的勞動力**｜**Service industries employ about 75% of workers**
- **製造業雇用約兩成**｜**Manufacturing employs about 20%**
- **自然資源雇用約半成，但出口貢獻很大**｜**Natural resources employ about 5%, but account for a large share of exports**

### 3. 重要產業

- **石油與天然氣**主要在 Alberta、Saskatchewan 與紐芬蘭外海｜**Oil and gas** come mainly from Alberta, Saskatchewan and offshore Newfoundland
- **礦業**產出黃金、鎳與鉀肥｜**Mining** produces gold, nickel and potash
- **林業**集中在 BC、Quebec 與 Ontario｜**Forestry** is centred in BC, Quebec and Ontario
- **漁業**分布在大西洋與太平洋兩岸｜**Fishing** takes place on both the Atlantic and Pacific coasts
- **農業**以草原省的小麥與芥花為主｜**Agriculture** is dominated by Prairie wheat and canola
- **製造業**集中在 Ontario 與 Quebec，包括汽車與飛機｜**Manufacturing** is concentrated in Ontario and Quebec, including cars and aircraft
- **金融業**以 Toronto 為中心，那裡有多倫多證交所 TSX｜**Finance** is centred in Toronto, home of the Toronto Stock Exchange

### 4. 最大貿易夥伴

- **美國是加拿大最大的貿易夥伴**，雙邊貿易每年超過八千億加幣｜**The United States is Canada's largest trading partner**, with more than 800 billion dollars of trade a year
- 其次是**中國、英國、日本與墨西哥**｜Next come **China, the United Kingdom, Japan and Mexico**

### 5. 自由貿易協定（重要年份）

| 年份 | 協定 |
|---|---|
| 1989 | **加美自由貿易協定 CUSFTA** |
| 1994 | **NAFTA**（加、美、墨） |
| **2020** | **USMCA / CUSMA**（取代 NAFTA） |
| 2017 | **CETA** 加歐自由貿易協定 |
| 2018 | **CPTPP** 跨太平洋夥伴協定 |

### 6. 加拿大的國際地位

- **G7**，七大工業國組織｜**The G7**, the group of seven industrialized democracies
- **G20**，二十國集團｜**The G20**
- **OECD**，經濟合作暨發展組織｜**The OECD**
- **APEC**，亞太經濟合作｜**APEC**, Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation
- **NATO**，北大西洋公約組織｜**NATO**
- **聯合國 UN**｜**The United Nations**
- **Commonwealth**，大英國協｜**The Commonwealth**
- **La Francophonie**，法語國家組織｜**La Francophonie**

### 7. 工會與勞工保護

- 工會在加拿大是合法的｜Labour unions are legal in Canada
- 勞工在依法協商破裂後有**罷工權**｜Workers have **the right to strike** once lawful bargaining has failed
- 最低工資與工作安全由**省層級**規範｜Minimum wages and safety standards are set by the **provinces**

### 8. 重要加拿大企業

- **Bombardier** 製造飛機與火車｜**Bombardier** makes aircraft and trains
- **Magna International** 生產汽車零件｜**Magna International** makes auto parts
- **CGI** 是資訊科技公司｜**CGI** is an information-technology company
- 五大銀行是 **RBC、TD、Scotiabank、BMO、CIBC**｜The five big banks are **RBC, TD, Scotiabank, BMO and CIBC**
- **Loblaws** 是最大的雜貨連鎖｜**Loblaws** is the largest grocery chain

### 9. Ontario 經濟重點

- Ontario 是**全國經濟規模最大的省**｜Ontario has **the largest economy of any province**
- Windsor 到 Toronto 的走廊是**汽車製造中心**｜The Windsor-to-Toronto corridor is the **heart of the auto industry**
- Toronto 的 Bay Street 是加拿大的**金融中心**｜Toronto's Bay Street is Canada's **financial centre**
- Toronto 到 Waterloo 之間是**科技走廊**｜The Toronto-Waterloo corridor is Canada's **technology belt**

## 答案與解析

1. **兩者都是**。｜**Both.**
2. **服務業、製造業、自然資源業**。｜**Service industries, manufacturing industries and natural resources industries.**
3. **服務業**，約 **75%**。｜**Service industries**, about **75%.**
4. **美國**。｜**The United States.**
5. **CUSFTA，Canada-US Free Trade Agreement**。｜**CUSFTA, the Canada-United States Free Trade Agreement.**
6. **USMCA，加拿大稱 CUSMA**。｜**USMCA, known in Canada as CUSMA.**
7. **加拿大與歐盟**。｜**Canada and the European Union.**
8. **Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership**，共十一國。｜**The Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership**, with eleven member countries.
9. 代表加拿大是 **世界主要工業化民主國家之一**。｜It means Canada is **one of the world's leading industrialized democracies.**
10. **UN、NATO、G7、G20、OECD、APEC、Commonwealth、La Francophonie**，任四個。｜**The UN, NATO, the G7, the G20, the OECD, APEC, the Commonwealth and La Francophonie** — any four.
11. **Alberta、Saskatchewan、Newfoundland and Labrador**。｜**Alberta, Saskatchewan, and Newfoundland and Labrador.**
12. 主要是 **小麥與芥花**。｜Mainly **wheat and canola.**
13. **TSX，Toronto Stock Exchange**，在 **Toronto**。｜**The TSX, the Toronto Stock Exchange**, in **Toronto.**
14. **Bombardier**。｜**Bombardier.**
15. 加拿大的 **金融中心**，相當於華爾街。｜Canada's **financial centre**, its Wall Street.
16. **有**，在依法協商破裂之後。｜**Yes**, once lawful bargaining has failed.
17. **省政府**。｜**The provinces.**
18. **法語國家組織**；加拿大有大量法裔人口，特別是 Quebec 與 New Brunswick。｜**The organization of French-speaking countries**; Canada has a large francophone population, especially in Quebec and New Brunswick.
19. **製造業**，生產汽車零件。｜**Manufacturing** — auto parts.
20. **RBC、TD、Scotiabank、BMO、CIBC** 任兩家。｜**RBC, TD, Scotiabank, BMO or CIBC** — any two.
