## 今日重點

### 1. 加拿大的政體：3 個關鍵詞（必背）

> **Canada is a constitutional monarchy, a parliamentary democracy, and a federal state.**

- **君主立憲制**：國王是元首，但必須遵守憲法｜**Constitutional monarchy**: the King is head of state but is bound by the Constitution
- **議會民主**：人民選出代表組成國會，由國會多數黨組政府｜**Parliamentary democracy**: voters elect members of Parliament, and the party with the most seats forms the government
- **聯邦制**：聯邦、省、領地與市政府各有權責｜**Federal state**: powers are divided among federal, provincial, territorial and municipal governments

### 2. 政府的 3 個層級

| 層級 | 範圍 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| **Federal 聯邦** | 全國事務 | 國防、外交、貨幣、移民、刑法 |
| **Provincial 省 / Territorial 領地** | 各省事務 | 教育、健保、自然資源、財產法 |
| **Municipal 市政 / Local** | 地方事務 | 公車、垃圾、警消、地方稅 |

加拿大有十個省、三個領地，以及數千個市政府。｜Canada has ten provinces, three territories and thousands of municipalities.

### 3. 國會 Parliament 的 3 部分（超常考）

> **The Sovereign, the Senate, and the House of Commons.**

#### a) The Sovereign 君主

- 現任君主是 **King Charles III**｜The Sovereign today is **King Charles III**
- 在加拿大由 **總督 Governor General** 代表｜He is represented in Canada by the **Governor General**
- 現任總督是 **Mary Simon**，**史上首位原住民總督**，2021 年上任｜The Governor General is **Mary Simon**, the **first Aboriginal person to hold the office**, appointed in 2021
- 總督任期大約五年｜The Governor General usually serves about five years

#### b) The Senate 參議院

- 大約 **105 位參議員**｜There are about **105 senators**
- **由總理提名、總督任命**，不是民選｜They are **appointed by the Governor General on the advice of the Prime Minister**, not elected
- 可任職到 **75 歲**｜They may serve until age **75**
- 代表各地區、少數族裔與不同的利益｜They represent regions, minorities and different interests
- 常被稱為「**冷靜複審之院 Chamber of Sober Second Thought**」｜It is known as the **Chamber of Sober Second Thought**

#### c) The House of Commons 眾議院

- 目前有 **338 個席次**，席次按人口分配｜It has **338 seats**, distributed by population
- **由人民直接選出**｜Members are **elected directly by the people**
- 任期最長四年｜A Parliament lasts at most four years
- 議員稱為 **MP，Member of Parliament**｜A member is called an **MP, a Member of Parliament**
- 席次最多的政黨黨魁出任 **總理 Prime Minister**｜The leader of the party with the most seats becomes **Prime Minister**

### 4. 立法流程：一個法案怎麼變法律（必背）

1. **一讀 First Reading**：在眾議院提出法案｜**First reading**: the bill is introduced in the House of Commons
2. **二讀 Second Reading**：辯論法案的原則｜**Second reading**: the principle of the bill is debated
3. **委員會審查 Committee Stage**：逐條檢視｜**Committee stage**: the bill is examined clause by clause
4. **報告階段 Report Stage**：報告修改內容｜**Report stage**: the amendments are reported back
5. **三讀 Third Reading**：表決｜**Third reading**: the House votes
6. **送參議院重複同樣程序**｜**The Senate repeats the same steps**
7. **御准 Royal Assent**：由總督代表君主簽署｜**Royal assent**: the Governor General signs on the Sovereign's behalf
8. **正式成為加拿大的法律**｜**The bill becomes the law of Canada**

### 5. 行政分支

- **總理 Prime Minister** 是政府首長，也是多數黨黨魁｜The **Prime Minister** is the head of government and leader of the governing party
- **內閣 Cabinet** 由總理任命，成員多半是議員｜The **Cabinet** is chosen by the Prime Minister, usually from among MPs
- **內閣部長** 各自負責一個部會｜**Cabinet ministers** each run a department
- **公務員體系 Public Service** 負責執行政策｜The **public service** carries out the work of government

### 6. 司法分支

司法部分在 Day 10 詳談。｜The courts are covered on Day 10.

### 7. 省級政府架構（並行對照）

| 聯邦 | 省 |
|---|---|
| 君主 → Governor General 總督 | 君主 → Lieutenant Governor 省督 |
| Prime Minister 總理 | Premier 省長 |
| House of Commons | Legislative Assembly（多數省）/ National Assembly（Quebec） |
| MP（聯邦議員） | MLA / MPP（Ontario）/ MNA（Quebec） |

### 8. Ontario 重點（你的省份）

- **首府是 Toronto**｜The **capital is Toronto**
- **省督 Lieutenant Governor 是 Edith Dumont**，2023 年起，是**首位出任此職的法裔安省人**｜The **Lieutenant Governor is Edith Dumont**, since 2023, the **first Franco-Ontarian** in the post
- **省長 Premier 是 Doug Ford**，進步保守黨，2018 年起，2022 與 2025 連任｜The **Premier is Doug Ford** of the Progressive Conservatives, in office since 2018 and re-elected in 2022 and 2025
- 省議會是 **Legislative Assembly of Ontario**，議員稱 **MPP**｜The legislature is the **Legislative Assembly of Ontario**, and members are **MPPs**
- 執政黨是安省進步保守黨｜The governing party is the Progressive Conservative Party of Ontario
- 官方反對黨黨魁是 Marit Stiles，新民主黨，2023 年起｜The Leader of the Opposition is Marit Stiles of the NDP, since 2023

## 答案與解析

1. **Canada is a constitutional monarchy, a parliamentary democracy, and a federal state.**｜**Canada is a constitutional monarchy, a parliamentary democracy, and a federal state.**
2. **聯邦、省與領地、市政**。｜**Federal, provincial or territorial, and municipal.**
3. **省**。｜**Provincial.**
4. **聯邦**。｜**Federal.**
5. **君主、參議院、眾議院**。｜**The Sovereign, the Senate and the House of Commons.**
6. **King Charles III**。｜**King Charles III.**
7. **總督 Governor General**。｜**The Governor General.**
8. **Mary Simon**，**史上首位原住民總督**，2021 年上任。｜**Mary Simon**, the **first Aboriginal Governor General**, appointed in 2021.
9. **由總理提名、總督任命**；可任職到 **75 歲**。｜**Appointed by the Governor General on the Prime Minister's advice**, serving until age **75.**
10. **338 席**，任期最長 **四年**。｜**338 seats**, and a term of at most **four years.**
11. **MP，Member of Parliament**。｜**MP, Member of Parliament.**
12. 一讀、二讀、委員會、報告、三讀，送參議院重複，最後**御准**。｜First reading, second reading, committee, report, third reading, the same again in the Senate, then **royal assent.**
13. **御准**，由 **總督** 代表君主簽署。｜**Royal assent**, given by the **Governor General** on the Sovereign's behalf.
14. **由眾議院席次最多的政黨黨魁出任**。｜**The leader of the party with the most seats in the House of Commons.**
15. **省督 Lieutenant Governor**。｜**The Lieutenant Governor.**
16. **MPP，Member of Provincial Parliament**。｜**MPP, Member of Provincial Parliament.**
17. **Toronto**。｜**Toronto.**
18. **Doug Ford**，**進步保守黨 Progressive Conservative**。｜**Doug Ford**, of the **Progressive Conservatives.**
19. **Edith Dumont**，**首位法裔安省人省督**，2023 年起。｜**Edith Dumont**, the **first Franco-Ontarian Lieutenant Governor**, since 2023.
20. Quebec 叫 **National Assembly**，議員稱 **MNA**；其他省叫 **Legislative Assembly**。｜Quebec's is the **National Assembly** and its members are **MNAs**; elsewhere it is the **Legislative Assembly.**
