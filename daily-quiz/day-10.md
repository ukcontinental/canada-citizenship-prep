## 今日重點

### 1. 法治 Rule of Law（核心觀念）

> **Government and citizens, including the most powerful, are all bound by the law.**

- **沒有人可以凌駕於法律之上**｜**No one is above the law**
- 個人與政府受同一套法律約束｜Individuals and governments are bound by the same laws
- 法律必須清楚、公開，而且對所有人一體適用｜Laws must be clear, public, and applied to everyone alike

### 2. 法律的核心原則（常考）

1. **無罪推定**：未經定罪之前都是無辜的｜**Presumption of innocence**: everyone is innocent until proven guilty
2. **公正審判**：由獨立的法官審理｜**The right to a fair trial** before an independent judge
3. **沉默權**：不必自證其罪｜**The right to remain silent** — no one must incriminate themselves
4. **速審權**：案件應在合理時間內審理｜**The right to be tried within a reasonable time**
5. **司法獨立**：法官不受政府或政黨指揮｜**Judicial independence**: judges take no orders from government or parties

### 3. 警察 Police

加拿大有三層警力：｜Canada has three levels of policing:

| 警力 | 範圍 |
|---|---|
| **Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP)** 皇家騎警 | 聯邦警察，也在多數省份擔任省警；加拿大象徵之一 |
| **Provincial Police 省警** | 只有 Ontario（OPP）、Quebec（SQ）、Newfoundland 自有省警 |
| **Municipal Police 市警** | 大城市自有警隊，例如 **Toronto Police Service** |

被警察攔下時要保持冷靜、配合，但你有**保持沉默與聯絡律師**的權利。｜If the police stop you, stay calm and co-operate — but you have the right **to remain silent and to speak to a lawyer**.

### 4. 法院系統 The Courts

| 層級 | 名稱 | 職能 |
|---|---|---|
| **最高** | **Supreme Court of Canada** | 9 名法官；最終上訴；解釋憲法 |
| 上訴 | Court of Appeal（各省一個 ＋ 聯邦） | 審理上訴 |
| 高等 | Superior Court（各省名稱不同） | 重大刑事與民事 |
| 初級 | Provincial / Territorial Court | 小型刑事、家庭、青少年 |

- 最高法院有 **九位法官**，由 **首席法官 Chief Justice** 領導｜The Supreme Court has **nine judges**, led by the **Chief Justice**
- 法官由 **聯邦政府任命**｜Judges are **appointed by the federal government**

### 5. 陪審團 Jury

- 重大刑案由 **十二人陪審團** 評議｜Serious criminal cases are decided by a **jury of twelve**
- **服務陪審團是公民的責任**，被抽到不能無故拒絕｜**Jury service is a responsibility of citizenship**, and you may not refuse without good reason

### 6. Habeas Corpus

> 拉丁文意思是「你應提交此人」——被拘留的人可以要求出庭，挑戰拘留是否合法。｜The Latin means "you must produce the body" — a detained person may go before a court and challenge the detention.

### 7. Charter 與司法的關係

- 有了 1982 年憲章，法院可以審查 **政府的行為是否違憲**｜Since the 1982 Charter the courts may review **whether government action is constitutional**
- 違憲的法律，法院可以宣告無效｜A law that breaches the Charter can be struck down

### 8. 加拿大特殊原則

- **司法獨立**：法官受憲法保障，不能被輕易免職｜**Judicial independence**: judges are protected by the Constitution and cannot easily be removed
- **法律之前人人平等**：不分種族、性別、宗教、語言或年齡｜**Equality before the law**, regardless of race, sex, religion, language or age

## 答案與解析

1. **政府與人民，包括最有權力的人，全都受法律約束**，沒有人凌駕於法律之上。｜**Governments and citizens, including the most powerful, are all bound by the law**; no one is above it.
2. **無罪推定、公正審判、沉默權、速審權、司法獨立**，任三項。｜**Presumption of innocence, a fair trial, the right to silence, a timely trial, and judicial independence** — any three.
3. **未經證明有罪之前，任何人都是無辜的**。｜**Everyone is innocent until proven guilty.**
4. **聯邦的 RCMP、省警、市警**。｜**The RCMP federally, provincial police, and municipal police.**
5. **Royal Canadian Mounted Police 皇家騎警**。｜**The Royal Canadian Mounted Police.**
6. **Ontario、Quebec、Newfoundland and Labrador**。｜**Ontario, Quebec, and Newfoundland and Labrador.**
7. **Supreme Court of Canada**，共 **九位法官**。｜**The Supreme Court of Canada**, with **nine judges.**
8. **聯邦政府**。｜**The federal government.**
9. **十二人**。｜**Twelve.**
10. **責任**。｜**A responsibility.**
11. **被拘留的人可以要求出庭，挑戰拘留是否合法**。｜**The right of a detained person to challenge the detention before a court.**
12. 它讓法院可以 **審查政府的行為是否違憲**。｜It lets the courts **review whether government action is constitutional.**
13. **不能**；這是 **司法獨立** 原則。｜**No** — that is **judicial independence.**
14. **是**，憲章第 15 條明文保障。｜**Yes**, in section 15 of the Charter.
15. **沉默權**，你不必自證其罪。｜**The right not to incriminate yourself.**
16. **Toronto Police Service**。｜**The Toronto Police Service.**
17. **OPP，Ontario Provincial Police**。｜**OPP, the Ontario Provincial Police.**
18. **Sûreté du Québec，簡稱 SQ**。｜**The Sûreté du Québec, or SQ.**
19. **保持沉默，以及聯絡律師**。｜**To remain silent, and to speak to a lawyer.**
20. **宣告該法律違憲的部分無效**。｜**Strike down the part of the law that breaches the Charter.**
