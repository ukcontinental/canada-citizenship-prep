# Day 9：聯邦選舉 + 主要政黨

## 今日重點

### 1. 投票權

- **滿 18 歲的加拿大公民** 就可以投票｜**Canadian citizens aged 18 or over** may vote
- 投票**既是權利也是責任**｜Voting is **both a right and a responsibility**
- 選務由獨立機構 **Elections Canada** 負責｜Elections are run by the independent agency **Elections Canada**

### 2. 聯邦大選的基本規則

- 一屆國會最長 **四年**，戰時可例外延長｜A Parliament lasts at most **four years**, with an exception in wartime
- 公民投票選出自己選區的 **MP**｜Voters elect the **MP** for their own riding
- 全國分為 **338 個選區**，英文叫 ridings 或 electoral districts｜The country is divided into **338 ridings**, also called electoral districts
- 每區選一位議員，**得票最多者當選，稱為簡單多數制**｜Each riding elects one member — **the candidate with the most votes wins, which is called first-past-the-post**

### 3. 大選後組政府

- 選後由 **眾議院席次最多的政黨** 黨魁，經總督邀請組閣｜After the election the Governor General invites the leader of **the party with the most seats** to form a government
- **多數政府**：席次超過半數｜A **majority government** holds more than half the seats
- **少數政府**：席次未過半，必須與其他政黨合作｜A **minority government** holds fewer, and must work with other parties

### 4. 主要聯邦政黨（必背 5 大政黨）

| 簡稱 | 全名 | 立場概述 |
|---|---|---|
| **Liberal** | Liberal Party of Canada 自由黨 | 中間偏左 |
| **Conservative** | Conservative Party of Canada 保守黨 | 中間偏右 |
| **NDP** | New Democratic Party 新民主黨 | 社會民主、勞工為主 |
| **Bloc Québécois** | Bloc Québécois 魁北克集團 | 僅在 Quebec 競選 |
| **Green** | Green Party of Canada 綠黨 | 環保優先 |

### 5. 反對黨機制

- 眾議院 **第二多席次的政黨** 就是 **官方反對黨**｜The party with **the second most seats** becomes the **Official Opposition**
- 它的黨魁稱為 **Leader of the Opposition**｜Its leader is the **Leader of the Opposition**
- 反對黨會組「**影子內閣 Shadow Cabinet**」對應每個部會｜The Opposition forms a **shadow cabinet** to mirror each department
- 反對黨的核心職責是 **讓政府向人民負責**｜Its central job is to **hold the government accountable**

### 6. 如何投票

- 投票前會收到 **投票通知卡 Voter Information Card**｜Before the vote you receive a **voter information card**
- 到指定的 **投票站 polling station** 投票｜You vote at your assigned **polling station**
- 要帶 **政府發行的有效身分證件**｜Bring **government-issued identification**
- 可以**提前投票、郵寄投票，或當天到投票站投**｜You may vote **at an advance poll, by mail, or in person on election day**

### 7. 法案如何成為法律（補強 Day 8）

> First → Second → Committee → Report → Third → Senate（重複）→ **Royal Assent**

只有 **眾議院** 可以提出涉及稅收與支出的 **財政法案 money bills**。｜Only the **House of Commons** may introduce **money bills**, which deal with taxes and spending.

### 8. 公民可以怎麼參與政治

- 加入政黨，或參與黨魁選舉｜Join a political party, or take part in choosing its leader
- 競選聯邦議員、省議員、市議員或市長｜Run for MP, MPP or MLA, city councillor or mayor
- 寫信給民意代表，或直接聯絡他們的選區辦公室｜Write to your elected representatives or contact their constituency office
- 投票、參加公眾諮詢、擔任陪審員｜Vote, take part in public consultations, and serve on a jury

### 9. Ontario 與 Toronto 的聯邦選舉小知識

- Ontario 在眾議院有 **121 席**，是席次最多的省｜Ontario holds **121 seats** in the House of Commons, more than any other province
- 在 Toronto 投票時，**MP 是聯邦議員、MPP 是省議員、市議員屬於市政層級**｜In Toronto your **MP is federal, your MPP is provincial, and your councillor is municipal**
- 考前記得上 elections.ca 查你選區的 MP 姓名｜Before the test, look up your riding's MP at elections.ca

## 答案與解析

1. **滿 18 歲的加拿大公民**。｜**Canadian citizens aged 18 or over.**
2. **Elections Canada**。｜**Elections Canada.**
3. **338 個選區**。｜**338.**
4. **簡單多數制 first-past-the-post**，每個選區得票最多的人當選。｜**First-past-the-post** — the candidate with the most votes in each riding wins.
5. **多數政府**是執政黨單獨過半；**少數政府**未過半，要靠其他黨才能通過法案。｜A **majority** government holds more than half the seats; a **minority** does not, and needs other parties to pass bills.
6. **總督 Governor General**。｜**The Governor General.**
7. **Liberal、Conservative、NDP、Bloc Québécois、Green**。｜**The Liberals, Conservatives, NDP, Bloc Québécois and Greens.**
8. **Bloc Québécois**。｜**The Bloc Québécois.**
9. **官方反對黨**，由眾議院席次第二多的政黨黨魁擔任，職銜是 **Leader of the Opposition**。｜**The Official Opposition**, led by the head of the party with the second most seats, titled **Leader of the Opposition.**
10. **影子內閣**，反對黨對應每個政府部會指派一位發言人。｜**A shadow cabinet** — Opposition critics assigned to match each government department.
11. **讓政府向人民負責**。｜**To hold the government accountable.**
12. **Voter Information Card**。｜**The voter information card.**
13. **政府發行的有效身分證件**，要能證明姓名與地址。｜**Government-issued ID** showing your name and address.
14. **提前投票**，在大選日之前開放的投票方式。｜**Voting at a poll open before election day.**
15. **四年**，戰時可延至五年。｜**Four years**, five in wartime.
16. **眾議院 House of Commons**。｜**The House of Commons.**
17. **御准 Royal Assent**，由總督代為簽署。｜**Royal assent**, given by the Governor General.
18. **加入政黨、競選公職、寫信給民代、參與公眾諮詢、擔任陪審員**，任三種。｜**Joining a party, running for office, writing to representatives, joining consultations, serving on a jury** — any three.
19. **121 席**，是全國最多的省。｜**121** — more than any other province.
20. **MP 代表聯邦，MPP 代表省**。｜**Your MP represents you federally; your MPP provincially.**
