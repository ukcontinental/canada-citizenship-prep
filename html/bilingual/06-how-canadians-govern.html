第 6 章：加拿大的政府制度 / How Canadians Govern Themselves

對應 PDF 第 28–29 頁。對應 day-08.md。

三個關鍵詞

English

There are three key facts about Canada’s system of government: our country is a federal state, a parliamentary democracy and a constitutional monarchy.

中文

加拿大政府制度有 3 個關鍵事實：

  1. 聯邦國家 (federal state)
  2. 議會民主 (parliamentary democracy)
  3. 君主立憲 (constitutional monarchy)

Federal State / 聯邦國家

English

There are federal, provincial, territorial and municipal governments in Canada. The responsibilities of the federal and provincial governments were defined in 1867 in the British North America Act, now known as the Constitution Act, 1867.

中文

加拿大有 聯邦、省、領地、市政 四級政府。聯邦與省的職權於 1867 年由《英屬北美法案》（今稱《1867 年憲法法》）所定義。

English

In our federal state, the federal government takes responsibility for matters of national and international concern. These include defence, foreign policy, interprovincial trade and communications, currency, navigation, criminal law and citizenship.

中文

聯邦政府負責 全國與國際事務：國防、外交、跨省貿易與通訊、貨幣、航行、刑法、公民身分。

English

The provinces are responsible for municipal government, education, health, natural resources, property and civil rights, and highways. The federal government and the provinces share jurisdiction over agriculture and immigration.

中文

省政府負責 市政府、教育、健保、自然資源、財產與民事權利、公路。農業與移民由聯邦與省共同管轄。

English

Federalism allows different provinces to adopt policies tailored to their own populations, and gives provinces the flexibility to experiment with new ideas and policies.

中文

聯邦制讓不同省份能因地制宜，並給予省份試行新想法與政策的彈性。

English

Every province has its own elected Legislative Assembly, like the House of Commons in Ottawa. The three northern territories, which have small populations, do not have the status of provinces, but their governments and assemblies carry out many of the same functions.

中文

每個省都有自己 民選的立法議會 (Legislative Assembly)，類似渥太華的眾議院。3 個北方領地人口較少，不具「省」的地位，但其政府與議會執行許多相同功能。

Parliamentary Democracy / 議會民主

English

In Canada’s parliamentary democracy, the people elect members to the House of Commons in Ottawa and to the provincial and territorial legislatures. These representatives are responsible for passing laws, approving and monitoring expenditures, and keeping the government accountable. Cabinet ministers are responsible to the elected representatives, which means they must retain the “confidence of the House” and have to resign if they are defeated in a non-confidence vote.

中文

在加拿大的議會民主下，人民選舉 聯邦眾議院 (House of Commons) 與 省/領地立法機構 的代表。這些代表負責 立法、批准並監督支出、讓政府向民眾負責。內閣大臣 (Cabinet ministers) 對民選代表負責——必須維持 「眾議院信任」，若 不信任投票 (non-confidence vote) 中敗北必須辭職。

English

Parliament has three parts: the Sovereign (Queen or King), the Senate and the House of Commons. Provincial legislatures comprise the Lieutenant Governor and the elected Assembly.

中文

國會 (Parliament) 由三部分組成：

  1. 君主 (the Sovereign，國王或女王)
  2. 參議院 (the Senate)
  3. 眾議院 (the House of Commons)

省議會則由 省督 (Lieutenant Governor) + 民選議會 組成。

English

In the federal government, the Prime Minister selects the Cabinet ministers and is responsible for the operations and policy of the government. The House of Commons is the representative chamber, made up of members of Parliament elected by the people, traditionally every four years. Senators are appointed by the Governor General on the advice of the Prime Minister and serve until age 75. Both the House of Commons and the Senate consider and review bills (proposals for new laws). No bill can become law in Canada until it has been passed by both chambers and has received royal assent, granted by the Governor General on behalf of the Sovereign.

中文

聯邦政府中：

  • 總理 (Prime Minister) 任命內閣，負責政府運作與政策
  • 眾議院 是代議機構，由民選議員 (MP) 組成，通常每 4 年改選
  • 參議員由總督依總理建議任命，任期至 75 歲
  • 任何法案 (bill) 必須兩院通過 + 總督代表君主御准 (royal assent) 才能成為法律

English

Living in a democracy, Canadian citizens have the right and the responsibility to participate in making decisions that affect them. It is important for Canadians aged 18 or more to participate in their democracy by voting in federal, provincial or territorial and municipal elections.

中文

生活在民主制度中，加拿大公民擁有參與決策的權利與責任。18 歲以上的加拿大人應該 透過在 聯邦、省/領地、市政 各級選舉中投票來參與民主。

Constitutional Monarchy / 君主立憲制

English

As a constitutional monarchy, Canada’s head of State is a hereditary Sovereign (Queen or King), who reigns in accordance with the Constitution: the rule of law. The Sovereign is a part of Parliament, playing an important, non-partisan role as the focus of citizenship and allegiance, most visibly during royal visits to Canada.

中文

作為君主立憲制，加拿大的 國家元首 (head of State) 是世襲君主——國王或女王——依據憲法（法治）統治。君主是國會的一部分，扮演 重要、非黨派 的角色，作為公民認同與效忠的焦點，尤其在皇室訪加時最為明顯。

English

Her Majesty is a symbol of Canadian sovereignty, a guardian of constitutional freedoms, and a reflection of our history. The Royal Family’s example of lifelong service to the community is an encouragement for citizens to give their best to their country. As Head of the Commonwealth, the Sovereign links Canada to 53 other nations that cooperate to advance social, economic and cultural progress.

中文

君主是 加拿大主權的象徵、憲法自由的守護者、我們歷史的反映。皇室終身服務社群的典範鼓勵公民盡力為國家貢獻。作為 大英國協元首，君主將加拿大連結到 其他 53 個合作推進社會、經濟、文化進步的國家。

English

Other constitutional monarchies include Denmark, Norway, Sweden, Australia, New Zealand, The Netherlands, Spain, Thailand, Japan, Jordan and Morocco.

中文

其他君主立憲國家包括 丹麥、挪威、瑞典、澳洲、紐西蘭、荷蘭、西班牙、泰國、日本、約旦、摩洛哥。

English

There is a clear distinction in Canada between the head of state—the Sovereign—and the head of government—the Prime Minister, who actually directs the governing of the country.

中文

加拿大 「國家元首」（君主） 與 「政府首長」（總理） 有明確區分——總理才是實際領導政府運作的人。

English

The Sovereign is represented in Canada by the Governor General, who is appointed by the Sovereign on the advice of the Prime Minister, usually for five years. In each of the ten provinces, the Sovereign is represented by the Lieutenant Governor, who is appointed by the Governor General on the advice of the Prime Minister, also normally for five years.

中文

君主在加拿大由 總督 (Governor General) 代表——由君主依總理建議任命，任期通常 5 年。在 10 個省中，君主由 省督 (Lieutenant Governor) 代表——由總督依總理建議任命，任期通常 5 年。

English

The interplay between the three branches of government—the Executive, Legislative and Judicial—which work together but also sometimes in creative tension, helps to secure the rights and freedoms of Canadians.

中文

三權（行政、立法、司法）之間的互動——既合作又有時有創造性張力——有助於保障加拿大人的權利與自由。

Provincial / Territorial Government / 省與領地政府

English

Each provincial and territorial government has an elected legislature where provincial and territorial laws are passed. The members of the legislature are called members of the Legislative Assembly (MLAs), members of the National Assembly (MNAs), members of the Provincial Parliament (MPPs) or members of the House of Assembly (MHAs), depending on the province or territory.

中文

每個省與領地都有 民選立法機構，通過省/領地法律。議員的稱呼依省/領地而不同：

稱呼 縮寫 適用地區
Member of the Legislative Assembly MLA 大多數省
Member of the National Assembly MNA Quebec
Member of the Provincial Parliament MPP Ontario
Member of the House of Assembly MHA Newfoundland and Labrador
English

In each province, the Premier has a role similar to that of the Prime Minister in the federal government, just as the Lieutenant Governor has a role similar to that of the Governor General. In the three territories, the Commissioner represents the federal government and plays a ceremonial role.

中文

每個省的 省長 (Premier) 在省級扮演如同聯邦總理的角色，省督 (Lieutenant Governor) 則類似總督。3 個領地 由 「專員 (Commissioner)」 代表聯邦政府，職務偏儀式性。

Other Levels of Government / 其他層級政府

Municipal Government / 市政府

English

Local or municipal government plays an important role in the lives of our citizens. Municipal governments usually have a council that passes laws called “by-laws” that affect only the local community. The council usually includes a mayor (or a reeve) and councillors or aldermen.

中文

地方/市政府 在公民生活中扮演重要角色。市政府通常有 理事會 (council) 通過 「附則 (by-laws)」——只影響當地社區。理事會通常包含 市長 (mayor)（或 reeve） 與 議員 (councillors / aldermen)。

English

Municipalities are normally responsible for urban or regional planning, streets and roads, sanitation (such as garbage removal), snow removal, firefighting, ambulance and other emergency services, recreation facilities, public transit and some local health and social services. Most major urban centres have municipal police forces.

中文

市政府通常負責 都市規劃、街道、清潔（如垃圾）、除雪、消防、救護車與其他緊急服務、休閒設施、大眾運輸，以及部分地方健康與社會服務。主要都市都有自己的市警隊。

First Nations Government / 第一民族政府

English

The First Nations have band chiefs and councillors who have major responsibilities on First Nations reserves, including housing, schools and other services.

中文

第一民族 (First Nations) 設有 族長 (band chiefs) 與議員，負責保留地內的房屋、學校、其他服務。
