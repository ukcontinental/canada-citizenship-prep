第 7 章：聯邦選舉 / Federal Elections

對應 PDF 第 30–33 頁。對應 day-09.md。

開場

English

Canadians vote in elections for the people they want to represent them in the House of Commons. In each election, voters may re-elect the same members of the House of Commons or choose new ones. Members of the House of Commons are also known as members of Parliament or MPs.

中文

加拿大人投票選出代表他們進入 眾議院 的人。每次選舉，選民可 連任原議員或選出新人。眾議員又稱 members of Parliament 或 MPs。

選舉週期

English

Under legislation passed by Parliament, federal elections must be held on the third Monday in October every four years following the most recent general election. The Prime Minister may ask the Governor General to call an earlier election.

中文

依國會通過的法律，聯邦大選須在前一次大選後第 4 年的 10 月第三個週一舉行。總理可請求總督提前舉行大選。

選區制度

English

Canada is divided into 308 electoral districts, also known as ridings or constituencies. An electoral district is a geographical area represented by a member of Parliament (MP). The citizens in each electoral district elect one MP who sits in the House of Commons to represent them, as well as all Canadians.

中文

加拿大分為 308 個選區 (electoral districts，亦稱 ridings 或 constituencies)。每個選區是一個 地理區，由一位 MP 代表。

注意：2021 年版 PDF 寫 308 席，2026 年實際聯邦眾議院已增為 338 席（人口增加導致重劃）。日常溝通使用 338 較準確；考試時若按 PDF 答 308 也可，但若考題問「最新數字」答 338。

English

Canadian citizens who are 18 years old or older may run in a federal election. The people who run for office are called candidates. There can be many candidates in an electoral district.

中文

18 歲以上的加拿大公民可參選聯邦選舉。參選者稱為 候選人 (candidates)。每個選區可以有多名候選人。

English

The people in each electoral district vote for the candidate and political party of their choice. The candidate who receives the most votes becomes the MP for that electoral district.

中文

每個選區的選民投給自己選擇的候選人與政黨。得票最多的候選人成為該選區的 MP（簡單多數制 first-past-the-post）。

投票資格

English

One of the privileges of Canadian citizenship is the right to vote. You are eligible to vote in a federal election or cast a ballot in a federal referendum if you are:

  • a Canadian citizen; and
  • at least 18 years old on voting day; and
  • on the voters’ list.
中文

投票是加拿大公民的 特權之一。符合 3 項條件 才有聯邦投票或公投資格：

  1. 加拿大公民
  2. 投票當日至少 18 歲
  3. 名字在選民名冊 (voters’ list) 上

Elections Canada 與選民資訊卡

English

The voters’ lists used during federal elections and referendums are produced from the National Register of Electors by a neutral agency of Parliament called Elections Canada. This is a permanent database of Canadian citizens 18 years of age or older who are qualified to vote in federal elections and referendums.

中文

聯邦選舉與公投使用的選民名冊由 「全國選民登記冊 (National Register of Electors)」 產生，由國會中立機構 加拿大選舉局 (Elections Canada) 維護——這是 18 歲以上加拿大公民選民資料的永久資料庫。

English

Once an election has been called, Elections Canada mails a voter information card to each elector whose name is in the National Register of Electors. The card lists when and where you vote and the number to call if you require an interpreter or other special services.

中文

大選宣布後，Elections Canada 寄發「選民資訊卡 (voter information card)」 給名冊上的每位選民——卡片載明 何時、何地投票，以及如需翻譯或特別服務的聯絡電話。

English

Even if you choose not to be listed in the National Register of Electors or do not receive a voter information card, you can still be added to the voters’ list at any time, including on election day. To vote either on election day or at advance polls, go to the polling station listed on your voter information card.

中文

即使您 未在登記冊上 或 沒收到資訊卡，您仍可在任何時候（包括選舉日當天）加入選民名冊。要在 選舉日 或 預先投票日 (advance polls) 投票，請依資訊卡所示前往投票所。

Secret Ballot / 無記名投票

English

Canadian law secures the right to a secret ballot. This means that no one can watch you vote and no one should look at how you voted. You may choose to discuss how you voted with others, but no one, including family members, your employer or union representative, has the right to insist that you tell them how you voted. Immediately after the polling stations close, election officers count the ballots and the results are announced on radio and television, and in the newspapers.

中文

加拿大法律保障 「無記名投票權 (right to a secret ballot)」：

  • 沒有人可以監視您投票
  • 沒有人可以查看您怎麼投
  • 您可以選擇與他人討論投誰，但 包括家人、雇主、工會代表，都無權強迫您告訴他們
  • 投票所關閉後，選務人員 立即計票，結果透過廣播、電視、報紙公布

After an Election / 選舉之後

English

Ordinarily, after an election, the leader of the political party with the most seats in the House of Commons is invited by the Governor General to form the government. After being appointed by the Governor General, the leader of this party becomes the Prime Minister.

中文

通常選舉後，眾議院席次最多政黨的黨魁被總督邀請組閣——獲總督任命後成為 總理 (Prime Minister)。

English

If the party in power holds at least half of the seats in the House of Commons, this is called a majority government. If the party in power holds less than half of the seats in the House of Commons, this is called a minority government.

中文
  • 過半 → 多數政府 (Majority Government)
  • 未過半 → 少數政府 (Minority Government)
English

The Prime Minister and the party in power run the government as long as they have the support or confidence of the majority of the MPs. When the House of Commons votes on a major issue such as the budget, this is considered a matter of confidence. If a majority of the members of the House of Commons vote against a major government decision, the party in power is defeated, which usually results in the Prime Minister asking the Governor General, on behalf of the Sovereign, to call an election.

中文

只要獲眾議院多數議員信任，總理與執政黨即可運作政府。重大議題（如預算）的表決視為信任案。若多數議員反對政府重大決定，執政黨即被擊敗——通常導致總理請求總督代表君主宣布大選。

English

The Prime Minister chooses the ministers of the Crown, most of them from among members of the House of Commons. Cabinet ministers are responsible for running the federal government departments. The Prime Minister and the Cabinet ministers are called the Cabinet and they make important decisions about how the country is governed. They prepare the budget and propose most new laws. Their decisions can be questioned by all members of the House of Commons.

中文

總理從 眾議員中選出大臣 (ministers of the Crown)，主管聯邦各部會。總理與內閣大臣合稱「內閣 (Cabinet)」，主管國家重大決策。他們編列預算、提出多數新法案。所有眾議員都可質詢內閣決策。

Opposition Parties / 反對黨

English

The other parties that are not in power are known as opposition parties. The opposition party with the most members of the House of Commons is the Official Opposition or Her Majesty’s Loyal Opposition. The role of opposition parties is to peacefully oppose or try to improve government proposals.

中文

不執政的政黨稱為 反對黨 (opposition parties)。眾議院席次最多的反對黨稱「官方反對黨 (Official Opposition)」或「陛下的忠誠反對黨 (Her Majesty’s Loyal Opposition)」。反對黨的角色是 和平地反對或試圖改進政府提案。

English

There are three major political parties currently represented in the House of Commons: the Conservative Party, the New Democratic Party, and the Liberal Party.

中文

PDF 列出三大主要政黨：保守黨 (Conservative Party)、新民主黨 (NDP)、自由黨 (Liberal Party)。

更新： 2026 年眾議院通常還有 Bloc Québécois（魁北克集團，僅 Quebec） 與 Green Party（綠黨） 等席次較少的政黨。考試會問 5 大政黨；以 Liberal、Conservative、NDP、Bloc Québécois、Green 為標準答案。

Voting Procedures During an Election Period / 選舉期間投票程序

# 步驟 中文說明
1 Voter Information Card 登記冊上的選民收到資訊卡，確認名字在名冊上，告知何時何地投票
2 如果沒收到資訊卡 致電當地選務辦公室或 Elections Canada（1-800-463-6868）確認名冊
3 Advance Polls / Special Ballot 無法或不想在選舉日投票，可參加預先投票或特殊郵寄投票
4 On election day 帶資訊卡與身分／地址證明前往資訊卡指定的投票所
5 Marking the ballot 在你選擇的候選人姓名旁的圓圈內畫 X
6 Voting is secret 在屏幕後標記，折好後交給選務人員
7 The ballot box 選務人員撕下選票編號，將選票交回給你投入票匭
8 The election results 投票所關閉後計票，結果在電視、Elections Canada 網站公布

📞 Elections Canada 熱線：1-800-463-6868
🌐 網站：www.elections.ca

投票身分證明

選舉日投票時請帶：

  • ✅ 政府核發、有姓名與地址的有效證件（如 BC/Ontario 駕照）
  • 或 兩件證件：一件有姓名（如健保卡）+ 一件有姓名與地址（如水電帳單）
  • 或在沒有證件時，由另一位有資格的選民「擔保 (vouch)」 您的身分
