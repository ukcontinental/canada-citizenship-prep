第 1 部分 — 公民、權利、責任（25 題）Chapter 1 — Citizenship, Rights and Responsibilities (25)

1. 加拿大入籍誓詞中效忠的對象是？答：加拿大國王查理三世，及其繼承人與後嗣。
1. To whom do you swear allegiance in the Oath of Citizenship? Answer: King Charles the Third, King of Canada, his heirs and successors.
2. 入籍誓詞特別承認哪三類原住民條約權利？答：第一民族、因紐特人、梅蒂人。
2. Which three Aboriginal peoples' treaty rights does the Oath specifically recognize? Answer: First Nations, Inuit and Métis.
3. 公民的 4 大基本自由依憲章是？答：良知／宗教自由；思想、信仰、意見與表達自由（含新聞）；和平集會自由；結社自由。
3. What are the four fundamental freedoms in the Charter? Answer: Freedom of conscience and religion; freedom of thought, belief, opinion and expression (including the press); freedom of peaceful assembly; freedom of association.
4. 公民的 6 大責任？答：守法、照顧自己與家庭、擔任陪審員、投票、協助社區、保護加拿大遺產與環境。
4. What are the six responsibilities of citizenship? Answer: Obeying the law, taking responsibility for oneself and one's family, serving on a jury, voting in elections, helping others in the community, and protecting and enjoying our heritage and environment.
5. 「Mobility Rights」指什麼？答：在加拿大任意居住、工作、自由進出國境並申請護照的權利。
5. What are Mobility Rights? Answer: The right to live and work anywhere in Canada, to enter and leave the country freely, and to apply for a passport.
6. 「Habeas Corpus」是什麼權利？答：被拘留者可挑戰拘留合法性的權利；來自英國普通法。
6. What right is habeas corpus? Answer: The right to challenge unlawful detention by the state; it comes from English common law.
7. 公民有沒有不工作的權利？答：沒有此項保障，但也不強迫做特定工作。找工作、努力工作被視為責任與加拿大價值。
7. Do citizens have a right not to work? Answer: There is no such guarantee, but no one is forced into a particular job. Getting a job and working hard is described as a responsibility and a Canadian value.
8. 加拿大男女在法律上是否平等？答：是。
8. Are men and women equal under Canadian law? Answer: Yes.
9. 強迫婚姻在加拿大是民事還是刑事？答：刑事——犯者依加拿大刑法嚴懲。
9. Is forced marriage a civil or a criminal matter in Canada? Answer: Criminal — those guilty are severely punished under Canada's criminal laws.
10. 加拿大 1982 年的最重要法律文件是什麼？答：《加拿大權利與自由憲章》，1982 年寫入憲法。
10. What is Canada's most important legal document of 1982? Answer: The Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms, entrenched in the Constitution in 1982.
11. Magna Carta 是哪一年的英國文件？答：1215 年。
11. In what year was Magna Carta signed in England? Answer: 1215.
12. 服務陪審團屬權利還是責任？答：責任——被傳喚時依法必須擔任。
12. Is serving on a jury a right or a responsibility? Answer: A responsibility — when called, you are legally required to serve.
13. 投票屬權利還是責任？答：兩者皆是——投票權伴隨投票的責任。
13. Is voting a right or a responsibility? Answer: Both — the right to vote comes with the responsibility to vote.
14. 永久居民可不可以投票？答：不可以——只有 18 歲以上且在選民名冊上的加拿大公民可以。
14. Can permanent residents vote in federal elections? Answer: No — only Canadian citizens aged 18 or older on the voters' list.
15. 加拿大法律來自哪三大傳統？答：英國不成文憲政、英國普通法、法國民法，加上國會與各省通過的法律。
15. What are the three traditions that Canadian law comes from? Answer: The unwritten British constitution, English common law, and the civil code of France, plus laws passed by Parliament and the provinces.
16. 「Rule of Law」是什麼意思？答：政府與人民都受法律約束，沒有人凌駕於法律之上。
16. What does the rule of law mean? Answer: Both government and people are bound by the law; no person or group is above the law.
17. 言論自由是否包含新聞自由？答：是——表達自由包含言論自由與新聞自由。
17. Does freedom of expression include freedom of the press? Answer: Yes — freedom of thought, belief, opinion and expression includes freedom of speech and of the press.
18. 多元文化遺產屬於權利還是責任？答：權利——憲章把多元文化列為加拿大傳統與身分的基本特徵。
18. Is multiculturalism a right or a responsibility? Answer: A right — the Charter lists multiculturalism as a fundamental characteristic of Canadian heritage and identity.
19. 守法屬於權利還是責任？答：責任。
19. Is obeying the law a right or a responsibility? Answer: A responsibility.
20. 你不可以做以下哪一項：（A）信仰自選（B）公開集會（C）拒絕守法（D）和平結社答：C——守法是每位公民的責任。
20. Which of these may you NOT do: (A) choose your religion, (B) assemble publicly, (C) refuse to obey the law, (D) associate peacefully? Answer: C — obeying the law is a responsibility of every citizen.
21. 「Equality before the law」中文是？答：法律之前人人平等。
21. What is 'equality before the law' in Chinese? Answer: Everyone is equal before the law.
22. 公民與永久居民的最大差別之一是什麼？答：投票權、競選公職、持加拿大護照。
22. What is one of the biggest differences between a citizen and a permanent resident? Answer: Citizens can vote, run for office and hold a Canadian passport.
23. 「協助社區」是公民責任嗎？答：是——數百萬志工無償幫助他人。
23. Is helping others in the community a responsibility of citizenship? Answer: Yes — millions of volunteers help others without pay.
24. 「保護加拿大遺產與環境」是責任還是權利？答：責任。
24. Is protecting Canada's heritage and environment a responsibility or a right? Answer: A responsibility.
25. 申請入籍時誓詞中說的「Constitution」包含哪一份文件？答：《加拿大權利與自由憲章》，其中承認並確認原住民與條約權利。
25. Which document does 'the Constitution' in the Oath include? Answer: The Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms, which recognizes and affirms Aboriginal and treaty rights.

第 2 部分 — 我們是誰、語言、原住民（20 題）Chapter 2 — Who We Are, Languages, Aboriginal Peoples (20)

1. 加拿大三大開國民族？答：原住民、法裔、英裔。
1. Who are Canada's three founding peoples? Answer: Aboriginal, French and British.
2. 原住民 3 大類別與占原住民人口比例？答：第一民族約 65%、梅蒂人 30%、因紐特人 4%。
2. What are the three groups of Aboriginal peoples and their share of the Aboriginal population? Answer: First Nations about 65%, Métis 30%, Inuit 4%.
3. 哪一族原住民住在北極區？答：因紐特人。
3. Which Aboriginal people live in the Arctic? Answer: The Inuit.
4. 哪一族原住民是歐裔與原住民混血？答：梅蒂人。
4. Which Aboriginal people are of mixed Aboriginal and European ancestry? Answer: The Métis.
5. 兩種官方語言？答：英語與法語。
5. What are the two official languages? Answer: English and French.
6. 官方語言法哪一年通過？答：1969 年。
6. In what year was the Official Languages Act passed? Answer: 1969.
7. 多元文化主義哪一年成為加拿大官方政策？答：1971 年。
7. In what year did multiculturalism become official policy? Answer: 1971.
8. 推動多元文化主義的總理是？答：皮埃爾・艾略特・杜魯道（老杜魯道）。
8. Which prime minister introduced multiculturalism? Answer: Pierre Elliott Trudeau.
9. 加拿大最大宗教傳統是？答：基督宗教——天主教最多，其次是新教。
9. What is the largest religious tradition in Canada? Answer: Christianity — Catholics are the largest group, followed by Protestants.
10. 加拿大目前人口大約多少？答：約 4,000 萬（2021 年版指南寫約 3,400 萬）。
10. What is Canada's population today? Answer: About 40 million (the 2021 guide says about 34 million).
11. 三大都會是？答：多倫多、蒙特婁、溫哥華。
11. What are Canada's three largest metropolitan areas? Answer: Toronto, Montreal and Vancouver.
12. 法裔人口大約多少？答：約 700 萬以法語為第一語言，多數住魁北克。
12. How many Francophones are there? Answer: About seven million speak French as their first language; most live in Quebec.
13. 哪個省以法文為唯一官方語言？答：魁北克。
13. Which province has French as its only official language? Answer: Quebec.
14. Acadians 是指什麼人？答：1604 年起在濱海省份定居的法國殖民者後代。
14. Who are the Acadians? Answer: Descendants of French colonists who began settling in the Maritimes in 1604.
15. Quebecers 是指什麼人？答：魁北克的居民，絕大多數說法語。
15. Who are Quebecers? Answer: The people of Quebec, the vast majority French-speaking.
16. 「Residential Schools」是什麼？答：聯邦政府強制原住民兒童入住、禁止母語與傳統文化、以同化為目的的學校，許多學生受苦。
16. What were residential schools? Answer: Schools where the federal government placed Aboriginal children to assimilate them; languages and culture were mostly prohibited and many students suffered.
17. 加拿大政府哪一年正式為寄宿學校道歉？由誰代表？答：2008 年；總理史蒂芬・哈珀。
17. When did the government apologize for residential schools, and who spoke? Answer: 2008; Prime Minister Stephen Harper.
18. 80% 的加拿大人住城市還是鄉村？答：城市。
18. Do most Canadians live in cities or rural areas? Answer: Cities — the majority live in urban areas.
19. 與原住民簽訂條約最早從哪一年開始？答：1763 年《皇家公告》奠定了條約談判的基礎。
19. When were treaties with Aboriginal peoples first guaranteed? Answer: The Royal Proclamation of 1763 established the basis for negotiating treaties.
20. 加拿大尊重宗教自由嗎？答：是——良知與宗教自由是基本自由。
20. Does Canada respect religious freedom? Answer: Yes — freedom of conscience and religion is a fundamental freedom.

第 3 部分 — 歷史（45 題）Chapter 3 — History (45)

1. 「Canada」一詞來自哪族語言？意思？答：易洛魁語——kanata 意為「村莊」。
1. Which language does the word 'Canada' come from, and what does it mean? Answer: Iroquoian — kanata means 'village'.
2. 第一批抵達北美的歐洲人是哪族？地點？年代？答：維京人；紐芬蘭的蘭塞奧茲牧草地；約一千年前。
2. Who were the first Europeans to reach North America, where, and when? Answer: The Vikings; L'Anse aux Meadows, Newfoundland; about 1,000 years ago.
3. 1497 探勘加拿大東岸的義裔英國探險家？答：約翰・卡伯特。
3. Which Italian explorer sailing for England mapped Canada's East Coast in 1497? Answer: John Cabot.
4. 1534/1535 探勘 St. Lawrence Gulf 的法國人？答：雅克・卡蒂亞。
4. Which Frenchman explored the Gulf of St. Lawrence in 1534–35? Answer: Jacques Cartier.
5. 1608 建立 Quebec City 者？被稱什麼？答：薩繆爾・德・尚普蘭；新法蘭西之父。
5. Who founded Quebec City in 1608, and what is he called? Answer: Samuel de Champlain; the Father of New France.
6. 1670 成立的最大殖民貿易公司？由哪位英王授權？答：哈德遜灣公司；英王查理二世。
6. Which great trading company was chartered in 1670, and by which king? Answer: The Hudson's Bay Company; King Charles II.
7. 哈德遜灣公司的領地稱為？答：魯珀特地。
7. What was the Hudson's Bay Company's territory called? Answer: Rupert's Land.
8. 1759 戰役名稱與地點？答：亞伯拉罕平原戰役，魁北克市城外。
8. What was the battle of 1759, and where? Answer: The Battle of the Plains of Abraham, outside Quebec City.
9. 1759 英法兩將軍是誰？結果？答：沃爾夫（英）與蒙卡姆（法）；雙雙陣亡，英軍勝。
9. Who were the two commanders in 1759, and what was the result? Answer: Wolfe (British) and Montcalm (French); both were killed and the British won.
10. 1763 法國割讓加拿大的條約？答：1763 年《巴黎條約》。
10. By which treaty did France cede Canada in 1763? Answer: The Treaty of Paris, 1763.
11. 1774《Quebec Act》保留法裔哪三項權利？答：天主教信仰自由、擔任公職的權利、法國民法。
11. Which three rights did the Quebec Act of 1774 preserve for French Canadians? Answer: Religious freedom for Catholics, the right to hold public office, and French civil law.
12. 1775–83 北逃加拿大的英裔保皇黨稱？答：效忠派／聯合帝國效忠派。
12. What were the British loyalists who fled north in 1775–83 called? Answer: Loyalists, or United Empire Loyalists.
13. Loyalists 大致逃了多少人？答：超過四萬人。
13. Roughly how many Loyalists fled to Canada? Answer: More than 40,000.
14. 1812 戰爭加拿大對抗哪國？答：美國。
14. Which country did Canada fight in the War of 1812? Answer: The United States.
15. 1812「Saviour of Upper Canada」是誰？答：少將艾薩克・布洛克爵士。
15. Who was the 'Saviour of Upper Canada' in 1812? Answer: Major-General Sir Isaac Brock.
16. 1812 與英軍結盟的原住民首領是？答：酋長特庫姆塞。
16. Which First Nations chief allied with the British in 1812? Answer: Chief Tecumseh.
17. Laura Secord 做了什麼事？答：步行 30 公里向英軍通報美軍進攻，促成比佛丹戰役勝利。
17. What did Laura Secord do? Answer: She walked 30 km to warn the British of an American attack, helping win the Battle of Beaver Dams.
18. 1813 美軍焚毀的城市？今天叫什麼？答：約克；今天的多倫多。
18. Which city did the Americans burn in 1813, and what is it called today? Answer: York; today Toronto.
19. 1814 英軍焚毀的建築是？答：華盛頓特區的白宮。
19. Which building did the British burn in 1814? Answer: The White House in Washington, D.C.
20. 1834 大英帝國通過什麼？答：全帝國廢除奴隸制度（1833 年）。
20. What did the British Empire do in 1833–34? Answer: It abolished slavery throughout the Empire (1833).
21. Underground Railroad 是什麼？答：協助美國黑奴秘密逃往加拿大的基督教反奴隸網絡。
21. What was the Underground Railroad? Answer: A Christian anti-slavery network that helped American slaves escape to Canada.
22. 1837–38 兩位叛亂領袖？答：威廉・萊昂・麥肯齊（上加拿大）與路易－約瑟夫・帕皮諾（下加拿大）。
22. Who were the two rebel leaders of 1837–38? Answer: William Lyon Mackenzie (Upper Canada) and Louis-Joseph Papineau (Lower Canada).
23. 1840 統一上下加拿大的法案？答：1840 年《聯合法案》。
23. Which Act united Upper and Lower Canada in 1840? Answer: The Act of Union, 1840.
24. 1848/49 達成責任政府的雙人組？答：羅伯特・鮑德溫與路易－伊波利特・拉方丹。
24. Which pair achieved responsible government in 1848–49? Answer: Robert Baldwin and Louis-Hippolyte La Fontaine.
25. 1867/7/1 邦聯誕生的法律文件名稱？答：1867 年《英屬北美法案》。
25. Which law created Confederation on July 1, 1867? Answer: The British North America Act, 1867.
26. 1867 建國 4 個省？答：安大略、魁北克、新斯科細亞、新布藍瑞克。
26. Which four provinces formed Canada in 1867? Answer: Ontario, Quebec, Nova Scotia and New Brunswick.
27. 加拿大首任總理？答：約翰・A・麥克唐納爵士。
27. Who was Canada's first prime minister? Answer: Sir John A. Macdonald.
28. Dominion 字源出自聖經？答：《詩篇》72 篇 8 節——「從這海直到那海」。
28. Where does the word 'Dominion' come from? Answer: Psalm 72:8 — 'dominion from sea to sea'.
29. 國徽箴言中文？答：從海到海。
29. What is Canada's motto in Chinese? Answer: A mari usque ad mare — from sea to sea.
30. Manitoba 與 NWT 加入年份？答：1870 年。
30. When did Manitoba and the Northwest Territories join? Answer: 1870.
31. BC 加入年份？答：1871 年。
31. When did British Columbia join? Answer: 1871.
32. PEI 加入年份？答：1873 年。
32. When did Prince Edward Island join? Answer: 1873.
33. Yukon 加入年份？答：1898 年。
33. When was Yukon Territory created? Answer: 1898.
34. Alberta、Saskatchewan 加入年份？答：1905 年。
34. When did Alberta and Saskatchewan join? Answer: 1905.
35. Newfoundland and Labrador 加入年份？答：1949 年。
35. When did Newfoundland and Labrador join? Answer: 1949.
36. Nunavut 成立年份？答：1999 年。
36. When was Nunavut created? Answer: 1999.
37. CPR 完工年份？答：1885 年。
37. When was the Canadian Pacific Railway completed? Answer: 1885.
38. CPR 多少華工參與？哪一年道歉？哪位總理？答：約 15,000 人；2006 年由總理史蒂芬・哈珀為人頭稅道歉。
38. How many Chinese workers built the CPR, and when and by whom was the apology made? Answer: About 15,000; the apology for the Head Tax came in 2006 from Prime Minister Stephen Harper.
39. Métis 領袖 Louis Riel 兩次抵抗年份？最終下場？答：1869–70 紅河；1885 西北叛亂；以叛國罪處絞。
39. When were Louis Riel's two uprisings, and what happened to him? Answer: Red River 1869–70 and the North-West Rebellion 1885; he was hanged for high treason.
40. 1916 首個給予婦女省級投票權的省？答：曼尼托巴。
40. Which province first gave women the provincial vote, in 1916? Answer: Manitoba.
41. 1921 首位女性 MP？答：艾格妮絲・麥克費爾。
41. Who was the first woman MP, in 1921? Answer: Agnes Macphail.
42. 1929 婦女被法律承認為「人」的案件名稱？答：「人格案」（Persons Case），由 Famous Five 打贏。
42. What was the 1929 case that recognized women as 'persons'? Answer: The Persons Case, won by the Famous Five.
43. WWI 加拿大服役/陣亡人數？答：超過 60 萬人服役；6 萬人陣亡。
43. How many Canadians served and died in the First World War? Answer: More than 600,000 served; 60,000 were killed.
44. 1917 加拿大民族認同誕生的戰役？答：維米嶺。
44. Which 1917 battle is seen as the birth of Canadian national identity? Answer: Vimy Ridge.
45. WWII 加拿大何時對德宣戰？陣亡人數約？D-Day 加軍負責灘頭？答：1939 年 9 月；約 44,000 人陣亡；朱諾海灘。
45. When did Canada declare war on Germany, how many died, and which D-Day beach did Canadians take? Answer: September 1939; about 44,000 killed; Juno Beach.

第 4 部分 — 現代加拿大（25 題）Chapter 4 — Modern Canada (25)

1. 加拿大何時加入 UN？答：1945 年。
1. When did Canada join the United Nations? Answer: 1945.
2. 加拿大何時加入 NATO？答：1949 年。
2. When did Canada join NATO? Answer: 1949.
3. 韓戰加拿大派兵多少？答：超過 26,000 人；500 人陣亡、1,000 人受傷。
3. How many Canadians served in the Korean War? Answer: More than 26,000; 500 died and 1,000 were wounded.
4. Lester Pearson 何時獲諾貝爾和平獎？答：1957 年。
4. When did Lester Pearson win the Nobel Peace Prize? Answer: 1957.
5. 因什麼貢獻獲獎？答：1956 年蘇伊士危機時提出聯合國維和部隊。
5. For what contribution did Pearson win the prize? Answer: Proposing the UN peacekeeping force during the 1956 Suez Crisis.
6. 國旗哪一年啟用？答：1965 年。
