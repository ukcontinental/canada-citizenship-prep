官方練習題Study Questions

公民身分的基本要求之一，是證明你對加拿大有足夠的了解。公民考試用來評估你對加拿大以及身為加拿大公民的權利與責任的了解。所有公民考試的題目都根據這本學習指南提供的資訊出題。你會被問到指南中提到的事實和概念。下面的題目和公民考試中的題目類似。用這些題目來準備你的考試。所有答案都可以在這本學習指南裡找到。
One of the basic requirements of citizenship is to demonstrate that you have adequate knowledge of Canada. The citizenship test is used to assess your knowledge of Canada and the rights and responsibilities of being a citizen in Canada. All the citizenship test questions are based on information provided in this study guide. You will be asked about facts and ideas presented in the guide. The questions below are similar to the questions that are found on the citizenship test. Use these questions to prepare for your test. All the answers can be found in this study guide.

選擇題範例Sample multiple-choice questions

公民的三項責任是什麼？a）效忠加拿大、回收報紙、在海軍、陸軍或空軍服役。b）遵守法律、為自己和家人負責、擔任陪審員。c）學習兩種官方語言、在選舉中投票、加入工會。d）購買加拿大產品、擁有自己的事業、少用水。答案是 b。
What are three responsibilities of citizenship? a) Being loyal to Canada, recycling newspapers, serving in the navy, army or air force. b) Obeying the law, taking responsibility for oneself and one's family, serving on a jury. c) Learning both official languages, voting in elections, belonging to a union. d) Buying Canadian products, owning your own business, using less water. The answer is b.
國殤紀念日的罌粟花有什麼意義？a）紀念我們的君主伊莉莎白二世女王。b）慶祝聯邦成立。c）向已故的總理致敬。d）紀念至今在戰爭中服役或犧牲的加拿大人。答案是 d。
What is the meaning of the Remembrance Day poppy? a) To remember our Sovereign, Queen Elizabeth II. b) To celebrate Confederation. c) To honour prime ministers who have died. d) To remember the sacrifice of Canadians who have served or died in wars up to the present day. The answer is d.
國會議員是怎麼產生的？a）由聯合國任命。b）由各省省長挑選。c）由他們所在選區的選民選出。d）由地主和警察局長選出。答案是 c。
How are members of Parliament chosen? a) They are appointed by the United Nations. b) They are chosen by the provincial premiers. c) They are elected by voters in their local constituency (riding). d) They are elected by landowners and police chiefs. The answer is c.
