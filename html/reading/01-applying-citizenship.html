🎧 01 申請公民身分 Applying for Citizenship

左中文、右英文，句對句對照。按段落左邊的 ▶ 朗讀該段；點任何一句從那句開始念；念到哪一句，中英文同時標黃。空白鍵＝暫停／繼續。

申請公民身分Applying for Citizenship

當你申請公民身分時，官員會查核你的身分狀態、確認你沒有被禁止申請，並確保你符合各項要求。你的申請可能需要好幾個月。在申請處理期間，請確保客服中心隨時有你正確的地址。
When you apply for citizenship, officials will check your status, verify that you are not prohibited from applying, and ensure that you meet the requirements. Your application may take several months. Please ensure that the Call Centre always has your correct address while your application is being processed.

如何用這本手冊準備公民考試How to use this booklet to prepare for the citizenship test

這本手冊會幫助你準備公民考試。你應該研讀這本指南。請朋友或家人幫你練習回答關於加拿大的問題。打電話給當地的學校或教育局、學院、社區中心，或為移民提供服務的地方組織，詢問公民課程的資訊。參加加拿大政府免費提供的英語或法語課程。
This booklet will help you prepare for the citizenship test. You should study this guide. Ask a friend or family member to help you practise answering questions about Canada. Call a local school or school board, a college, a community centre or a local organization that provides services to immigrants and ask for information on citizenship classes. Take English or French language classes, which the Government of Canada offers free of charge.

關於公民考試About the citizenship test

公民考試通常是筆試，但也可能是面試。考試會測驗公民身分的兩項基本要求：一是對加拿大以及公民權利與責任的了解，二是足夠的英語或法語能力。55 歲以上的成年申請人不需要參加公民考試。《公民法規》說明了如何判定你是否符合「了解加拿大」這項要求。所有公民考試的題目都根據《公民法規》列出的主題範圍出題，而所有需要的資訊都在這本學習指南裡。
The citizenship test is usually a written test, but it could be an interview. You will be tested on two basic requirements for citizenship: knowledge of Canada and of the rights and responsibilities of citizenship, and adequate knowledge of English or French. Adult applicants 55 years of age and over do not need to write the citizenship test. The Citizenship Regulations provide information on how your ability to meet the knowledge of Canada requirement is determined. All the citizenship test questions are based on the subject areas noted in the Citizenship Regulations, and all required information is provided in this study guide.

考試之後After the test

如果你通過考試並符合其他所有要求，你會收到一份「宣誓入籍出席通知」。這份文件會告訴你公民宣誓儀式的日期、時間和地點。
If you pass the test and meet all the other requirements, you will receive a Notice to Appear to Take the Oath of Citizenship. This document tells you the date, time and place of your citizenship ceremony.
在儀式上，你會宣讀公民誓詞、簽署誓詞表格，並領取你的加拿大公民證書。如果你沒有通過考試，你會收到一份通知，說明接下來的步驟。我們鼓勵你帶家人和朋友一起來慶祝這個時刻。
At the ceremony, you will take the Oath of Citizenship, sign the oath form, and receive your Canadian Citizenship Certificate. If you do not pass the test, you will receive a notification indicating the next steps. You are encouraged to bring your family and friends to celebrate this occasion.
