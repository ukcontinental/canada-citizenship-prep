5 大主要政黨 Major Political Parties

Liberal Party
自由黨
領袖：Mark Carney
現執政黨（2025-）Governing party (since 2025)
  • 中間偏左Centre-left
  • 支持多元文化、社會福利Multiculturalism, social programs
  • 現任 PM 政黨The Prime Minister's party
Conservative Party
保守黨
領袖：Pierre Poilievre
官方反對黨Official Opposition
  • 中間偏右Centre-right
  • 支持經濟自由、傳統價值Economic freedom, traditional values
  • 2003 由 PC 黨與聯盟黨合併Formed in 2003 by merging the PC and Alliance parties
New Democratic Party (NDP)
新民主黨
領袖：Don Davies (interim, 2025)
第三大黨Third party
  • 左派、勞工Left, labour
  • 推動社會醫療與社福Public health care and social programs
Bloc Québécois
魁北克集團
領袖：Yves-François Blanchet
只在魁北克省競選Runs only in Quebec
  • 主張魁北克利益與主權Quebec interests and sovereignty
  • 只在 Quebec 推候選人Candidates only in Quebec
Green Party
綠黨
領袖：Jonathan Pedneault & Elizabeth May
環保政黨Environmental party
  • 氣候變遷、永續政策Climate and sustainability
  • 規模較小，在西岸有支持Small party with West Coast support

投票資格 & 流程 Who Can Vote & Voting Procedures

🇨🇦
1. 公民身份1. Citizenship

必須是 Canadian Citizen（永久居民不能投）You must be a Canadian citizen (permanent residents cannot vote)

🎂
2. 年滿 18 歲2. Age 18

投票日當天滿 18At least 18 years old on voting day

📬
3. 選民資訊卡3. Voter information card

Elections Canada 寄出，列出投票時間地點Mailed by Elections Canada; lists when and where to vote

🏠
4. 到投票站4. Go to the polling station

帶卡片＋身分與地址證明Bring the card and proof of identity and address

✏️
5. 圈選候選人5. Mark your ballot

在候選人旁的圓圈畫 X，摺好交給工作人員Mark an X beside your candidate, fold it and hand it to the poll official

考試 5 大必背重點 Five Must-Know Facts

選舉週期Election cycle每 4 年 10 月第三個星期一（PM 可請 GG 提前）Third Monday in October every four years (the PM may ask the Governor General for an earlier election)
選區與議員Ridings and MPs每個選區選一位 MP；得票最多者當選Each riding elects one MP; the candidate with the most votes wins
誰當總理Who becomes PM眾議院席次最多政黨的領袖，由總督邀請組閣The leader of the party with the most seats, invited by the Governor General
多數／少數政府Majority / minority席次過半＝多數政府；不到一半＝少數政府At least half the seats = majority; fewer = minority government
