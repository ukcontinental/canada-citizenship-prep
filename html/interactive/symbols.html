🍁 加拿大國家象徵圖鑑 Canadian Symbols

象徵、鈔票、硬幣、國定假日——視覺整理一次到位。

10 大國家象徵 Ten National Symbols

🍁

楓葉 Maple Leaf

加拿大最著名的象徵Canada's best-known symbol

  • 1700 年代法裔加拿大人採用Adopted by French Canadians in the 1700s
  • 1850 年代起出現在軍服上On Canadian uniforms since the 1850s
  • 刻在陣亡將士墓碑上Carved on the headstones of fallen soldiers
🚩

國旗 National Flag

紅白楓葉旗The red-and-white maple leaf flag

  • 1965 年首次升起First raised in 1965
  • 紅白色來自皇家軍事學院旗；1921 起為國家代表色Red and white come from the Royal Military College flag; national colours since 1921
  • 前身 Red Ensign 用了約 100 年The Red Ensign served for about 100 years
👑

王權 / 君主 The Crown

King Charles IIIKing Charles III

  • 2022 年 9 月 Elizabeth II 過世，Charles III 即位Elizabeth II died in September 2022; Charles III became King
  • 王權象徵政府：國會、法院、警察、軍隊The Crown symbolizes government: Parliament, courts, police, the Forces
  • Governor General Mary Simon 代表君主Represented by Governor General Mary Simon
🦫

海狸 Beaver

5 分硬幣上的勤勞動物The industrious animal on the five-cent coin

  • 哈德遜灣公司的象徵Symbol of the Hudson's Bay Company
  • 1834 成為 St. Jean Baptiste Society 標誌Emblem of the St. Jean Baptiste Society from 1834
  • 也在 Saskatchewan、Alberta 省徽上Also on the arms of Saskatchewan and Alberta
🛡️

國徽與格言 Coat of Arms

一戰後採用Adopted after the First World War

  • 含英、法、蘇、愛象徵與紅楓葉Symbols of England, France, Scotland and Ireland, with red maple leaves
  • 格言 'A mari usque ad mare'＝從海到海Motto A mari usque ad mare = "from sea to sea"
  • 出現在鈔票、政府文件、公共建築Seen on dollar bills, government documents and public buildings
🎵

國歌 O Canada

1980 正式國歌Proclaimed the national anthem in 1980

  • 1880 首次在 Québec City 演唱First sung in Québec City in 1880
  • 2018 改 'in all of us command'Lyric changed to "in all of us command" in 2018
  • 皇家國歌是 'God Save the King'The Royal Anthem is "God Save the King"
🌹

Vimy Ridge Vimy Ridge

1917 加拿大關鍵戰役Canada's defining battle of 1917

  • 4 月 9 日 = Vimy DayApril 9 is Vimy Day
  • 「大英帝國突擊部隊」美名Won the reputation of "shock troops of the British Empire"
  • 法國 Vimy 有加拿大紀念碑The Vimy Memorial stands in France
🎖️

Order of Canada 加拿大勳章

1967 設立Established in 1967, the centennial

  • 加拿大自己的榮譽制度Canada's own honours system
  • 最高榮譽是維多利亞十字勳章（96 位加拿大人）The highest honour is the Victoria Cross (96 Canadians)
💰

Loonie & Toonie 1 元、2 元硬幣

Loonie 1987、Toonie 1996Loonie 1987, Toonie 1996

  • Loonie：1 元，刻 Loon 潛鳥Loonie: $1 with a loon
  • Toonie：2 元，刻 Polar Bear 北極熊Toonie: $2 with a polar bear
📜

Magna Carta 大憲章 1215

有序自由 800 年的起源Origin of 800 years of ordered liberty

  • 1215 年英國簽署Signed in England in 1215
  • Habeas Corpus 來自英國普通法Habeas corpus comes from English common law
  • 憲章序言承認上帝至高與法治The Charter's preamble recognizes the supremacy of God and the rule of law
