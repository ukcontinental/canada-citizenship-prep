⚖️ 加拿大司法系統 The Justice System

5 大法律原則 + 4 級法院階層 + RCMP 角色。

5 大法律原則 Five Principles of Canadian Law

法治

Rule of Law

沒人凌駕於法律之上，包括政府、法官、警察No one is above the law, including governments, judges and the police

無罪推定

Presumption of Innocence

被證明有罪前人人無辜Everyone is innocent until proven guilty

正當程序

Due Process

政府必須尊重每個人依法享有的所有權利The government must respect all the legal rights a person is entitled to

人身保護令

Habeas Corpus

挑戰國家非法拘留的權利，來自英國普通法The right to challenge unlawful detention, from English common law

法律之下的自由

Freedom under the Law

法律提供秩序與和平解決糾紛的方式Laws provide order and a peaceful way to settle disputes

皇家騎警 RCMP（Royal Canadian Mounted Police）

🐎 加拿大國家警察

  • 聯邦警察Federal police在全加拿大執行聯邦法律Enforces federal laws throughout Canada
  • 省警察Provincial police在 Ontario、Quebec 以外的省與地區擔任省警Serves as provincial police everywhere except Ontario and Quebec
  • 市鎮警察Municipal police也在一些市鎮服務；各省都有市警察局Also serves some municipalities; every province has municipal police departments
  • 象徵Symbol紅色制服、Stetson 帽、Musical Ride——加拿大最知名象徵之一Red Serge, Stetson hat, Musical Ride — one of Canada's best-known symbols
  • 歷史History1873 由 Macdonald 成立 North West Mounted Police，總部 ReginaFounded in 1873 as the North West Mounted Police; headquartered in Regina
  • 你的權利Your rights可以質疑警察的服務或行為，幾乎所有警力都有申訴程序You may question the police about their service or conduct; almost all forces have a complaint process
