Day 13：加拿大五大地理區、10 省 3 領地（Ontario 加強）

對應官方章節：Canada’s Regions 建議閱讀：PDF p.49–p.60

今日重點

1. 加拿大基本地理

  • 加拿大是世界第二大國家，僅次於俄羅斯Canada is the second-largest country in the world, after Russia
  • 三大洋環繞：東邊大西洋、北邊北冰洋、西邊太平洋Three oceans border it: the Atlantic to the east, the Arctic to the north, the Pacific to the west
  • 與美國共享世界最長的陸地邊界It shares the longest land border in the world with the United States
  • 加拿大首都是 Ottawa，位於 Ontario 省The capital of Canada is Ottawa, in the province of Ontario

2. 五大地理區（必背）

區域 英文 包含的省 / 領地
大西洋區 Atlantic Provinces Newfoundland and Labrador、PEI、Nova Scotia、New Brunswick

草原區 Prairie Provinces Manitoba、Saskatchewan、Alberta
西海岸 West Coast British Columbia

5. 各省與領地的小知識

  • Quebec 法裔最多；蒙特婁是世界第二大法語城市，僅次於巴黎Quebec has the most francophones; Montreal is the second-largest French-speaking city after Paris
  • Ontario 人口最多、經濟最大；尼加拉瀑布在此Ontario has the largest population and economy; Niagara Falls is here
  • PEI 是最小的省，《清秀佳人》的故鄉，靠聯邦大橋連上本土PEI is the smallest province, home of Anne of Green Gables, linked by the Confederation Bridge
  • Nova Scotia 的 Halifax 是大西洋區最大港，1917 年曾發生大爆炸Nova Scotia's Halifax is the region's biggest port, site of the 1917 explosion
  • New Brunswick 是唯一的官方雙語省New Brunswick is the only officially bilingual province
  • Manitoba 是 Métis 文化中心；Winnipeg 有「西部門戶」之稱Manitoba is the heart of Métis culture; Winnipeg is the Gateway to the West
  • Saskatchewan 是小麥之省；Regina 有皇家騎警的訓練學院Saskatchewan is the wheat province; Regina has the RCMP training academy
  • Alberta 是石油重鎮，境內有洛磯山脈的 Banff 與 Jasper 國家公園Alberta is oil country, with Banff and Jasper national parks in the Rockies
  • BC 是太平洋門戶；Vancouver 主辦 2010 年冬季奧運BC is the Pacific gateway; Vancouver hosted the 2010 Winter Olympics
  • Yukon 有 1896 年的克朗代克淘金潮，以及加拿大最高峰 Mount LoganYukon had the Klondike Gold Rush of 1896 and Canada's highest mountain, Mount Logan
  • NWT 以天然氣、礦業和極光聞名The Northwest Territories are known for gas, mining and the northern lights
  • Nunavut 以因紐特人為主，面積最大而人口最少Nunavut is mostly Inuit, the largest territory with the smallest population

7. 加拿大其他常考地理

  • 最高峰是 Yukon 的 Mount LoganThe highest mountain is Mount Logan in Yukon
  • 最大島是 Nunavut 的 Baffin IslandThe largest island is Baffin Island in Nunavut
  • 加拿大盾 Canadian Shield 是覆蓋大半國土的古老岩盤The Canadian Shield is the ancient rock that covers much of the country
  • 五大湖中有四個與美國共享，只有 Lake Michigan 完全在美國境內Four of the Great Lakes are shared with the United States; only Lake Michigan lies entirely in the U.S.

今日驗收題（25 題；含 Ontario 加強）

Part A — 全國地理（10 題）

  1. 加拿大是世界第幾大國？Where does Canada rank in size among countries?
  2. 加拿大首都是哪個城市？位於哪一省？What is Canada's capital, and in which province?
  3. 加拿大鄰接的三大洋是哪三個？Which three oceans border Canada?
  4. 加拿大分成哪五大地理區？What are Canada's five regions?
  5. Atlantic Provinces 包含哪四個省？Which four provinces are the Atlantic Provinces?
  6. Central Canada 包含哪兩個省？Which two provinces make up Central Canada?
  7. Prairie Provinces 包含哪三個省？Which three are the Prairie provinces?
  8. West Coast 是哪一個省？Which province is the West Coast?
  9. 北方有哪三個領地？What are the three northern territories?
  10. 加拿大最高峰是哪一座？在哪個領地？What is Canada's highest mountain, and where is it?

Part B — 省份首府（10 題）

  1. Newfoundland and Labrador 的首府是哪裡？What is the capital of Newfoundland and Labrador?
  2. Nova Scotia 的首府是哪裡？What is the capital of Nova Scotia?
  3. New Brunswick 的首府是哪裡？它有什麼與眾不同之處？What is New Brunswick's capital, and what makes the province unique?
  4. Quebec 的首府是哪裡？What is the capital of Quebec?
  5. Manitoba 的首府是哪裡？What is the capital of Manitoba?
  6. Saskatchewan 的首府是哪裡？哪一支警隊的總部在這？What is Saskatchewan's capital, and which police force trains there?
  7. Alberta 的首府是哪裡？境內有哪兩個國家公園？What is Alberta's capital, and which two national parks are there?
  8. BC 的首府是哪裡？2010 冬奧在哪個城市舉辦？What is BC's capital, and which city hosted the 2010 Winter Olympics?
  9. Yukon 的首府是哪裡？What is the capital of Yukon?
  10. Nunavut 哪一年成立？首府在哪？When was Nunavut created, and what is its capital?

Part C — Ontario 省題（5 題）

  1. Ontario 現任省長是誰？屬於哪一黨？Who is Ontario's Premier, and from which party?
  2. Ontario 現任省督是誰？她的歷史意義是什麼？Who is Ontario's Lieutenant Governor, and why is it historic?
  3. Ontario 的省議員縮寫是什麼？What is the short form for a member of Ontario's legislature?
  4. Ontario 的首府與加拿大首都分別是哪兩個城市？What is the capital of Ontario, and what is the capital of Canada?
  5. Ontario 境內哪個瀑布舉世聞名？它在哪兩個湖之間？Which world-famous falls are in Ontario, and between which two lakes?

  1. 世界第二大，僅次於俄羅斯。The second largest, after Russia.
  2. Ottawa，位於 Ontario。Ottawa, in Ontario.
  3. 大西洋、北冰洋、太平洋。The Atlantic, the Arctic and the Pacific.
  4. 大西洋區、中央區、草原區、西海岸、北方領地。The Atlantic Provinces, Central Canada, the Prairies, the West Coast and the North.
  5. Newfoundland and Labrador、PEI、Nova Scotia、New Brunswick。Newfoundland and Labrador, Prince Edward Island, Nova Scotia and New Brunswick.
  6. Quebec 與 Ontario。Quebec and Ontario.
  7. Manitoba、Saskatchewan、Alberta。Manitoba, Saskatchewan and Alberta.
  8. British Columbia。British Columbia.
  9. Yukon、Northwest Territories、Nunavut。Yukon, the Northwest Territories and Nunavut.
  10. Mount Logan，在 Yukon。Mount Logan, in Yukon.
  11. St. John&#x27;s。St. John's.
  12. Halifax。Halifax.
  13. Fredericton；它是唯一的官方雙語省。Fredericton; it is the only officially bilingual province.
  14. Quebec City。Quebec City.
  15. Winnipeg。Winnipeg.
  16. Regina；皇家騎警 RCMP 的訓練學院在這裡。Regina; the RCMP training academy is there.
  17. Edmonton；Banff 與 Jasper 國家公園。Edmonton; Banff and Jasper.
  18. Victoria；冬奧在 Vancouver 舉辦。Victoria; the Olympics were in Vancouver.
