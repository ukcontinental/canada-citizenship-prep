Day 14：期末模擬考（仿真考試 + 綜合大考）

⚠️ 考試規則（兩段）

第一段：真實考試模擬（必做）

  • ⏰ 30 分鐘30 minutes
  • 📋 20 題，聯邦 18 題加 Ontario 省題 2 題20 questions — 18 federal and 2 on Ontario
  • 📵 不翻書、不查網、不問人No book, no internet, no help
  • ✅ 通過標準 18／20，比真考的 75% 嚴格，留安全邊際Pass at 18 out of 20 — stricter than the real 75%, to leave a margin

第二段：綜合大考（拉高熟度）

  • ⏰ 60 分鐘60 minutes
  • 📋 40 題，涵蓋 Day 1 到 13 全範圍40 questions covering everything from Day 1 to Day 13
  • ✅ 通過標準 33／40Pass at 33 out of 40

兩段都過，你就有上考場的實力了。Pass both and you are ready for the real test.

第一段：真實考試模擬（20 題）

  1. 入籍誓詞中效忠的國王是誰？Which king do you swear allegiance to?
  2. 加拿大的政體可以用哪三個詞描述？用一句答完。Describe Canada's system of government in one sentence.
  3. 加拿大三大開國民族是哪三類？Who are the three founding peoples?
  4. 加拿大三類原住民是什麼？What are the three Aboriginal groups?
  5. 1867 年邦聯時的四個建國省份是哪四個？Which four provinces formed Confederation in 1867?
  6. 加拿大首任總理是誰？Who was the first prime minister?
  7. 1812 戰爭中被稱為「Saviour of Upper Canada」的將領是誰？Who was called the Saviour of Upper Canada?
  8. 1917 年哪一場戰役被視為加拿大民族認同的誕生？Which 1917 battle is seen as the birth of the nation?
  9. 現行國旗哪一年啟用？In what year was the current flag first raised?
  10. 國徽箴言是什麼意思？What does the national motto mean?
  11. 加拿大國會由哪三個部分組成？What are the three parts of Parliament?
  12. 眾議院總共多少席？How many seats are in the House of Commons?
  13. 五大主要聯邦政黨，任寫四個。Name four of the five main federal parties.
  14. 最高法院共有多少位法官？How many judges sit on the Supreme Court?
  15. 六大公民責任，任寫三條。Name three responsibilities of citizenship.
  16. 加拿大最大的貿易夥伴是哪一國？Who is Canada's largest trading partner?
  17. 加拿大屬於 G7 還是 G20？Is Canada in the G7 or the G20?
  18. 11 月 11 日是什麼節日？What day is November 11?
  19. Ontario 現任省長是誰？哪一黨？Who is the Premier of Ontario, and from which party?
  20. Ontario 的首府是哪個城市？省議員的縮寫是什麼？What is Ontario's capital, and what are its legislators called?

第二段：綜合大考（40 題）

Part A — 公民、權利、原住民、語言（8 題）

  1. 憲章保障的四大基本自由是什麼？What are the four fundamental freedoms?
  2. 加拿大兩種官方語言是什麼？官方語言法哪一年通過？What are the two official languages, and when was the Official Languages Act passed?
  3. 多元文化主義哪一年成為官方政策？哪位總理推動？When did multiculturalism become official policy, and under whom?
  4. 「Habeas Corpus」是什麼權利？What right is habeas corpus?
  5. 2008 年政府對哪一群體道歉？Whom did the government apologize to in 2008?
  6. 1988 年政府對哪一群體道歉？Whom did the government apologize to in 1988?
  7. 何時對華工人頭稅道歉？哪位總理？When was the head-tax apology, and by which prime minister?
  8. 配偶虐待與強迫婚姻在加拿大屬於民事還是刑事？Are spousal abuse and forced marriage civil or criminal?

Part B — 歷史（8 題）

  1. 「Canada」一詞來自哪個原住民語言？意思是什麼？What language does "Canada" come from, and what does it mean?
  2. 1670 年由英王查理二世授權成立的貿易公司是哪一家？Which trading company did Charles II charter in 1670?
  3. 1759 年亞伯拉罕平原戰役的兩位將軍是誰？結果如何？Who were the two generals in 1759, and what happened?
  4. 1774 年《Quebec Act》保留法裔哪三項權利？Which three rights did the Quebec Act keep?
  5. 加拿大邦聯是哪一天？建國四省是哪四個？What date was Confederation, and which four provinces?
  6. 加拿大首位法裔總理是誰？Who was the first French-Canadian prime minister?
  7. 太平洋鐵路哪一年完工？對 BC 有什麼意義？When was the CPR finished, and what did it mean for BC?
  8. 1949 年加入成為第十省的是哪一省？Which province joined as the tenth in 1949?

Part C — 婦女與原住民投票權（4 題）

  1. 第一個給婦女省級投票權的省份與年份？Which province gave women the provincial vote first, and when?
  2. 多數加拿大婦女取得聯邦投票權是哪一年？When did most Canadian women get the federal vote?
  3. Quebec 婦女取得省級投票權是哪一年？When did women in Quebec get the provincial vote?
  4. 原住民不必放棄條約身分即可投票是哪一年？When could Aboriginal people vote without losing treaty status?

Part D — 政府、選舉、司法（10 題）

  1. 現任總督是誰？她的歷史意義？Who is the Governor General, and why is it historic?
  2. 參議員如何產生？任職到幾歲？How are senators chosen, and until what age?
  3. 立法流程的最後一步叫什麼？由誰簽署？What is the final step of lawmaking, and who signs?
  4. 多數政府與少數政府差別在哪？What is the difference between majority and minority government?
  5. 哪一個政黨只在 Quebec 競選？Which party runs only in Quebec?
  6. 「Official Opposition」是什麼？主要職責是什麼？What is the Official Opposition, and what does it do?
  7. 加拿大三層警力是什麼？What are the three levels of policing?
  8. 最高法院有幾位法官？由誰任命？How many Supreme Court judges are there, and who appoints them?
  9. 重大刑案的陪審團人數是多少？How many jurors hear a serious criminal case?
  10. 司法系統的核心原則，任寫三個。Name three core principles of the justice system.

Part E — 象徵、節日、貨幣、經濟、地理（10 題）

  1. 加拿大兩個官方國民運動是什麼？What are the two national sports?
  2. 國歌哪一年成為官方國歌？皇室國歌是什麼？When did O Canada become official, and what is the royal anthem?
  3. 真相與和解日是哪一天？What date is Truth and Reconciliation Day?
  4. 十元鈔票上的人物是誰？她的歷史意義？Who is on the ten-dollar bill, and why?
  5. Loonie 是多少錢？上面是什麼動物？What is the loonie worth, and what is on it?
  6. 三大產業類別是什麼？服務業佔就業比例多少？What are the three types of industry, and what share of jobs is services?
  7. 2020 年取代 NAFTA 的協定簡稱是什麼？What replaced NAFTA in 2020?
  8. 加拿大五大地理區是哪五個？What are Canada's five regions?
  9. 加拿大首都是哪個城市？位於哪一省？What is Canada's capital, and in which province?
  10. Ontario、Quebec、Saskatchewan、Alberta 的首府分別是哪裡？Name the capitals of Ontario, Quebec, Saskatchewan and Alberta.
