Day 5：加拿大史 ③（邦聯後 → WWII）

對應官方章節：Canada’s History（後段） 建議閱讀：PDF p.21–p.24

今日重點

1. 加拿大太平洋鐵路 Canadian Pacific Railway (CPR)

  • 1885 年完工，把東岸和 British Columbia 連起來Completed in 1885, it linked the east to British Columbia
  • 修這條鐵路是 BC 加入邦聯的條件Building it was the condition on which BC joined Confederation
  • 約一萬五千名華工參與興建，許多人死於危險的工程About 15,000 Chinese workers built it, and many died doing dangerous work
  • 鐵路完工後，政府對華人移民課徵 人頭稅 Head TaxOnce the railway was done, the government imposed a head tax on Chinese immigrants
  • 2006 年總理 Stephen Harper 代表政府正式道歉In 2006 Prime Minister Stephen Harper apologized on behalf of the government

2. Métis 抵抗與 Louis Riel

  • Louis Riel 是 Métis 的領袖Louis Riel was the leader of the Métis
  • 他領導 1869 年紅河抵抗，促成 Manitoba 在 1870 年加入邦聯He led the Red River Resistance of 1869, which brought Manitoba into Confederation in 1870
  • 1885 年西北叛亂 戰敗，他被處以絞刑He was defeated in the North-West Rebellion of 1885 and hanged
  • 他至今仍是加拿大歷史上既受爭議又被紀念的人物He remains both a controversial and a celebrated figure in Canadian history

3. 婦女投票權（必背順序）

年份 事件
1916 Manitoba 首先給予婦女省級投票權（Nellie McClung 等推動）
1917 加拿大軍人的妻女取得聯邦投票權
1918 多數加拿大婦女取得聯邦投票權
1921 Agnes Macphail 成為史上首位女性國會議員 MP
1940 Quebec 婦女取得省級投票權（最晚）
1960 原住民 不必放棄條約身分即可投票

「The Famous Five」是五位婦女運動者，1929 年的「Persons Case」讓婦女在法律上被視為「人」，可被任命進入參議院。The Famous Five were five women whose 1929 Persons Case established that women are "persons" in law and may be appointed to the Senate.

4. WWI 第一次世界大戰 1914–1918

  • 超過六十萬名加拿大人服役More than 600,000 Canadians served
  • 約六萬人陣亡About 60,000 died
  • 1917 年維米嶺戰役 Vimy Ridge：加拿大軍團首次獨立作戰並獲勝，被視為民族認同的誕生At Vimy Ridge in 1917 the Canadian Corps fought as one and won — often called the birth of the nation
  • Sir Arthur Currie 是加拿大史上最傑出的軍人，指揮維米嶺Sir Arthur Currie, Canada's greatest soldier, commanded at Vimy
  • 軍醫 John McCrae 寫下名詩 《In Flanders Fields》，十一月的罌粟花由此而來The military doctor John McCrae wrote "In Flanders Fields", the source of the November poppy

5. 戰間期（1920–30 年代）

  • 1929 年開始的大蕭條 Great DepressionThe Great Depression began in 1929
  • 加拿大廣播公司 CBC 於 1936 年成立The Canadian Broadcasting Corporation was founded in 1936

6. WWII 第二次世界大戰 1939–1945

  • 加拿大在 1939 年 9 月自行對德宣戰，比英國晚一週，象徵外交獨立Canada declared war on Germany in September 1939, a week after Britain, as an independent country
  • 超過一百萬人服役More than a million Canadians served
  • 約四萬四千人陣亡About 44,000 died
  • 1942 年迪耶普戰役 Dieppe，加軍傷亡慘重At Dieppe in 1942 Canadian losses were heavy
  • 1943 年的義大利戰役The Italian campaign of 1943
  • 1944 年 6 月 6 日 D-Day 諾曼第登陸，加軍負責 Juno BeachOn D-Day, June 6, 1944, Canadians landed at Juno Beach
  • 1944 至 45 年解放荷蘭，荷蘭至今每年送鬱金香給加拿大Canadians liberated the Netherlands in 1944 and 1945; the Dutch still send tulips every year
  • 戰時 日裔加拿大人 被強制遷離 BC 沿岸並沒收財產During the war Japanese Canadians were moved away from the BC coast and their property was seized
  • 1988 年總理 Brian Mulroney 道歉並賠償In 1988 Prime Minister Brian Mulroney apologized and compensated them

7. 戰時婦女與工業

  • 戰時大量婦女進入工廠與軍隊Large numbers of women went into the factories and the forces during the war
  • 這段經驗改變了戰後婦女在職場的地位That experience changed women's place in the workforce after the war
