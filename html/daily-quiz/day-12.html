Day 12：加拿大經濟 Canada’s Economy

對應官方章節：Canada’s Economy 建議閱讀：PDF p.46–p.48

今日重點

1. 加拿大是「貿易大國 Trading Nation」

  • 貿易佔 GDP 六成以上Trade accounts for more than 60% of GDP
  • 加拿大是 G7 與 G20 的成員Canada belongs to both the G7 and the G20
  • 加拿大是 世界上最富裕的國家之一Canada is one of the wealthiest countries in the world

2. 三大產業類別（常考）

類別 英文 範例
服務業 Service Industries 交通、教育、健保、銀行、IT、政府服務
製造業 Manufacturing Industries 汽車、紙、食品、技術產品
自然資源業 Natural Resources Industries 林業、漁業、農業、能源、礦業
  • 服務業雇用約七成五的勞動力Service industries employ about 75% of workers
  • 製造業雇用約兩成Manufacturing employs about 20%
  • 自然資源雇用約半成，但出口貢獻很大Natural resources employ about 5%, but account for a large share of exports

3. 重要產業

  • 石油與天然氣主要在 Alberta、Saskatchewan 與紐芬蘭外海Oil and gas come mainly from Alberta, Saskatchewan and offshore Newfoundland
  • 礦業產出黃金、鎳與鉀肥Mining produces gold, nickel and potash
  • 林業集中在 BC、Quebec 與 OntarioForestry is centred in BC, Quebec and Ontario
  • 漁業分布在大西洋與太平洋兩岸Fishing takes place on both the Atlantic and Pacific coasts
  • 農業以草原省的小麥與芥花為主Agriculture is dominated by Prairie wheat and canola
  • 製造業集中在 Ontario 與 Quebec，包括汽車與飛機Manufacturing is concentrated in Ontario and Quebec, including cars and aircraft
  • 金融業以 Toronto 為中心，那裡有多倫多證交所 TSXFinance is centred in Toronto, home of the Toronto Stock Exchange

4. 最大貿易夥伴

  • 美國是加拿大最大的貿易夥伴，雙邊貿易每年超過八千億加幣The United States is Canada's largest trading partner, with more than 800 billion dollars of trade a year
  • 其次是中國、英國、日本與墨西哥Next come China, the United Kingdom, Japan and Mexico

5. 自由貿易協定（重要年份）

年份 協定
1989 加美自由貿易協定 CUSFTA
1994 NAFTA（加、美、墨）
2020 USMCA / CUSMA（取代 NAFTA）
2017 CETA 加歐自由貿易協定
2018 CPTPP 跨太平洋夥伴協定

6. 加拿大的國際地位

  • G7，七大工業國組織The G7, the group of seven industrialized democracies
  • G20，二十國集團The G20
  • OECD，經濟合作暨發展組織The OECD
  • APEC，亞太經濟合作APEC, Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation
  • NATO，北大西洋公約組織NATO
  • 聯合國 UNThe United Nations
  • Commonwealth，大英國協The Commonwealth
  • La Francophonie，法語國家組織La Francophonie

7. 工會與勞工保護

  • 工會在加拿大是合法的Labour unions are legal in Canada
  • 勞工在依法協商破裂後有罷工權Workers have the right to strike once lawful bargaining has failed
  • 最低工資與工作安全由省層級規範Minimum wages and safety standards are set by the provinces

8. 重要加拿大企業

  • Bombardier 製造飛機與火車Bombardier makes aircraft and trains
  • Magna International 生產汽車零件Magna International makes auto parts
  • CGI 是資訊科技公司CGI is an information-technology company
  • 五大銀行是 RBC、TD、Scotiabank、BMO、CIBCThe five big banks are RBC, TD, Scotiabank, BMO and CIBC
  • Loblaws 是最大的雜貨連鎖Loblaws is the largest grocery chain

9. Ontario 經濟重點

  • Ontario 是全國經濟規模最大的省Ontario has the largest economy of any province
  • Windsor 到 Toronto 的走廊是汽車製造中心The Windsor-to-Toronto corridor is the heart of the auto industry
  • Toronto 的 Bay Street 是加拿大的金融中心Toronto's Bay Street is Canada's financial centre
  • Toronto 到 Waterloo 之間是科技走廊The Toronto-Waterloo corridor is Canada's technology belt

今日驗收題（20 題）

  1. 加拿大是 G7 還是 G20 的成員？Is Canada in the G7, the G20, or both?
  2. 加拿大三大產業類別是什麼？What are the three main types of industry?
  3. 哪一類產業雇用最多勞動力？比例大約多少？Which employs the most workers, and what share?
  4. 加拿大最大的貿易夥伴是哪一國？Who is Canada's largest trading partner?
  5. 1989 年簽訂的加美貿易協定縮寫是什麼？What is the short name of the 1989 Canada-US trade deal?
  6. 2020 年取代 NAFTA 的協定簡稱是什麼？What replaced NAFTA in 2020?
  7. CETA 是哪兩方的貿易協定？Between whom is CETA?
  8. CPTPP 的全名是什麼？What does CPTPP stand for?
  9. 加拿大屬於 G7 代表什麼意義？What does G7 membership say about Canada?
  10. 加拿大屬於哪些主要國際組織？寫出四個。Name four international organizations Canada belongs to.
  11. 加拿大石油主要產在哪些省？Where is most of Canada's oil produced?
  12. 草原省主要生產什麼農產品？What do the Prairie provinces mainly grow?
  13. 加拿大證交所的縮寫是什麼？在哪個城市？What is Canada's stock exchange called, and where is it?
  14. 加拿大重要的飛機與火車製造公司是哪一家？Which Canadian company makes aircraft and trains?
  15. Toronto 的 Bay Street 被稱為加拿大的什麼？What is Bay Street known as?
  16. 加拿大工人有沒有罷工權？Do Canadian workers have the right to strike?
  17. 最低工資由哪一層政府規範？Which level of government sets the minimum wage?
  18. La Francophonie 是什麼組織？加拿大為什麼加入？What is La Francophonie, and why is Canada a member?
  19. Magna International 屬於哪一個產業？What industry is Magna International in?
  20. 加拿大五大銀行任寫兩家。Name two of Canada's five big banks.
答案與解析（點擊展開）
  1. 兩者都是。Both.
  2. 服務業、製造業、自然資源業。Service industries, manufacturing industries and natural resources industries.
  3. 服務業，約 75%。Service industries, about 75%.
  4. 美國。The United States.
  5. CUSFTA，Canada-US Free Trade Agreement。CUSFTA, the Canada-United States Free Trade Agreement.
  6. USMCA，加拿大稱 CUSMA。USMCA, known in Canada as CUSMA.
  7. 加拿大與歐盟。Canada and the European Union.
  8. Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership，共十一國。The Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership, with eleven member countries.
  9. 代表加拿大是 世界主要工業化民主國家之一。It means Canada is one of the world's leading industrialized democracies.
  10. UN、NATO、G7、G20、OECD、APEC、Commonwealth、La Francophonie，任四個。The UN, NATO, the G7, the G20, the OECD, APEC, the Commonwealth and La Francophonie — any four.
  11. Alberta、Saskatchewan、Newfoundland and Labrador。Alberta, Saskatchewan, and Newfoundland and Labrador.
  12. 主要是 小麥與芥花。Mainly wheat and canola.
  13. TSX，Toronto Stock Exchange，在 Toronto。The TSX, the Toronto Stock Exchange, in Toronto.
  14. Bombardier。Bombardier.
  15. 加拿大的 金融中心，相當於華爾街。Canada's financial centre, its Wall Street.
  16. 有，在依法協商破裂之後。Yes, once lawful bargaining has failed.
  17. 省政府。The provinces.
  18. 法語國家組織；加拿大有大量法裔人口，特別是 Quebec 與 New Brunswick。The organization of French-speaking countries; Canada has a large francophone population, especially in Quebec and New Brunswick.
  19. 製造業，生產汽車零件。Manufacturing — auto parts.
  20. RBC、TD、Scotiabank、BMO、CIBC 任兩家。RBC, TD, Scotiabank, BMO or CIBC — any two.
