Day 3：加拿大史 ①（早期 → 英法戰爭 1759）

對應官方章節：Canada’s History（前段） 建議閱讀：PDF p.14–p.16

今日重點

1. 名稱由來

  • 「Canada」來自 Iroquoian（易洛魁語）的「kanata」，意思是 「村莊 village」The name "Canada" comes from the Iroquoian word "kanata", meaning "village"
  • 1535 年探險家 Jacques Cartier 聽到原住民用這個字指稱聚落In 1535 the explorer Jacques Cartier heard the word used for a settlement

2. 早期探險（必記人名與年份）

年份 探險家 事蹟
~1000 Vikings 維京人 紐芬蘭 L’Anse aux Meadows 短暫定居
1497 John Cabot（義裔英國） 首位繪製加拿大東岸地圖的歐洲人
1534, 1535, 1541 Jacques Cartier（法國） 探勘聖羅倫斯灣，宣稱土地屬法王
1604 Pierre de Monts 建首個法裔殖民地 Port Royal（今新斯科細亞）
1608 Samuel de Champlain 創建 魁北克市 Québec City，被稱「新法蘭西之父」

3. 新法蘭西 New France（1608–1763）

  • 中心是 Québec CityIts centre was Québec City
  • 主要產業是 毛皮交易，特別是河狸皮The main industry was the fur trade, especially beaver pelts
  • 與 Algonquin、Huron 原住民結盟The French allied with the Algonquin and Huron peoples
  • 對手是與英國結盟的 Iroquois 易洛魁聯盟Their rivals were the Iroquois, who were allied with the British

4. 哈德遜灣公司 Hudson’s Bay Company（HBC）

  • 1670 年 由英王查理二世授權成立Chartered by King Charles II in 1670
  • 控制 Rupert&#x27;s Land 哈德遜灣集水區 的毛皮貿易It controlled the fur trade across Rupert's Land, the Hudson Bay watershed
  • 加拿大歷史上最古老的公司，至今仍在The oldest company in Canada, and it still exists today

5. 七年戰爭 / 英法戰爭

  • 1756 到 1763 年的全球戰爭A global war fought from 1756 to 1763
  • 1759 亞伯拉罕平原戰役 Battle of the Plains of AbrahamThe Battle of the Plains of Abraham, 1759
  • 戰場就在 Québec City 城外It was fought just outside Québec City
  • 英軍 General Wolfe 對法軍 General MontcalmThe British General Wolfe faced the French General Montcalm
  • 兩位將軍都陣亡Both generals died in the battle
  • 英國獲勝，新法蘭西落入英國手中Britain won, and New France passed into British hands
  • 1763 年《巴黎條約》Treaty of Paris：法國正式割讓加拿大給英國Under the Treaty of Paris of 1763 France formally ceded Canada to Britain

6. 英國統治後的安撫政策

  • 1774 年《魁北克法案》Quebec Act（極重要）The Quebec Act of 1774 — a key exam topic
  • 承認法語It allowed the French language
  • 承認天主教信仰It allowed the Catholic religion
  • 承認法國民法，用於私法事務It kept French civil law for private matters
  • 保障 Quebec 法裔身份不被同化It protected the French-Canadian identity from assimilation
  • 被視為現代加拿大寬容與多元的根基It is seen as the foundation of Canadian tolerance and diversity

7. 美國獨立的影響

  • 1775 到 1783 年的美國獨立戰爭The American Revolution, 1775 to 1783
  • 約四萬到五萬名 Loyalists 保皇黨 從美國北逃加拿大About 40,000 to 50,000 Loyalists fled north to Canada
  • 他們奠定了今天 New Brunswick 與 Ontario 的雛形They laid the foundations of today's New Brunswick and Ontario
  • 也帶來了大量英語人口They also brought a large English-speaking population

  6. 新法蘭西的主要經濟產業是什麼？What was the main industry of New France?
  7. 哪兩個原住民族與法國人結盟？Which two Aboriginal peoples allied with the French?
  8. 哈德遜灣公司哪一年成立？由誰授權？In what year was the Hudson's Bay Company founded, and who chartered it?

  11. 哪一年簽訂《巴黎條約》，法國正式割讓加拿大？In what year did the Treaty of Paris transfer Canada to Britain?
  12. 1774 年《Quebec Act》保留了法裔的哪三項權利？Which three French-Canadian rights did the Quebec Act of 1774 protect?

  14. 美國獨立戰爭中逃到加拿大的英裔保皇黨叫什麼名字？What were the British-American refugees of the Revolution called?
  15. 大約多少名 Loyalists 逃到加拿大？About how many Loyalists came to Canada?
  16. Loyalists 主要落腳今天的哪兩個省份？Which two of today's provinces did the Loyalists mainly settle?
  17. 1497 年探勘加拿大東岸的義裔英國探險家是誰？Who was the Italian-born explorer who sailed for England in 1497?
  18. 易洛魁 Iroquois 與哪個歐洲強權結盟？Which European power were the Iroquois allied with?
  19. 「Rupert&#x27;s Land」是哪間公司的領地？Which company held Rupert's Land?
  20. Quebec City 是哪一年、由誰建立？In what year and by whom was Québec City founded?

  1. Iroquoian 易洛魁語，意思是 「村莊 village」。The Iroquoian language; it means "village".
  2. 維京人 Vikings，約公元 1000 年在紐芬蘭的 L&#x27;Anse aux Meadows。The Vikings, around the year 1000, at L'Anse aux Meadows in Newfoundland.
  3. 1497 年。1497.
  4. Samuel de Champlain。Samuel de Champlain.
  5. 他建立魁北克市，讓法裔殖民地長期生存下來，奠定新法蘭西的基礎。He founded Québec City and made the French colony permanent, laying the foundation of New France.
  6. 毛皮交易，特別是河狸皮。The fur trade, especially beaver pelts.
  7. Algonquin 與 Huron。The Algonquin and the Huron.
  8. 1670 年，由 英王查理二世 Charles II 授權。1670, chartered by King Charles II.
  9. 亞伯拉罕平原戰役 Battle of the Plains of Abraham。The Battle of the Plains of Abraham.
  10. 英軍 General Wolfe、法軍 General Montcalm；兩人皆陣亡，英國獲勝。General Wolfe for Britain and General Montcalm for France; both died and Britain won.
  11. 1763 年。1763.
  12. 法語、天主教信仰、法國民法。The French language, the Catholic religion and French civil law.
  13. 它讓 Quebec 法裔保住語言、宗教與民法傳統，被視為加拿大寬容與多元的奠基石。It let French Canadians keep their language, religion and civil law, and is seen as the cornerstone of Canadian tolerance.
  14. Loyalists，也稱 United Empire Loyalists。The Loyalists, also called United Empire Loyalists.

  16. New Brunswick 與 Ontario。New Brunswick and Ontario.
  17. John Cabot，義大利名 Giovanni Caboto。John Cabot, born Giovanni Caboto.
  18. 英國。Britain.
  19. 哈德遜灣公司 Hudson&#x27;s Bay Company。The Hudson's Bay Company.
