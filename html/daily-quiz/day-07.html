Part A — 公民責任與權利（5 題）

  1. 加拿大六大公民責任是什麼？寫出三條即可得分。What are the six responsibilities of citizenship? Three will do.
  2. 入籍誓詞中效忠的國王是誰？Which king do you swear allegiance to in the oath?
  3. 憲章保障的四大基本自由是什麼？寫出三條。What are the four fundamental freedoms? Name three.
  4. 服務陪審團是權利還是責任？Is jury service a right or a responsibility?
  5. 「Habeas Corpus」是什麼？What is habeas corpus?

Part B — 我們是誰（5 題）

  1. 加拿大三大開國民族是哪三個？Who are the three founding peoples?
  2. 原住民的三大類別是什麼？What are the three Aboriginal groups?
  3. 加拿大的兩種官方語言是什麼？What are the two official languages?
  4. 哪一年通過《官方語言法》？In what year was the Official Languages Act passed?
  5. 多元文化主義哪一年成為官方政策？In what year did multiculturalism become official policy?

Part C — 加拿大歷史 ①（7 題）

  1. 「Canada」一詞來自哪個原住民語言？意思是什麼？What language does "Canada" come from, and what does it mean?
  2. 1608 年建立 Quebec City 的法國探險家是誰？Which French explorer founded Québec City in 1608?
  3. 哈德遜灣公司哪一年成立？In what year was the Hudson's Bay Company founded?
  4. 1759 年亞伯拉罕平原戰役的英法兩位將軍是誰？結果如何？Who were the generals at the Plains of Abraham in 1759, and who won?
  5. 1763 年哪一份條約讓法國割讓加拿大？Which 1763 treaty gave Canada to Britain?
  6. 1774 年《Quebec Act》保留了法裔哪三項權利？Which three rights did the Quebec Act protect?
  7. 美國獨立戰爭後北逃加拿大的英裔保皇黨叫什麼？What were the refugees who came north after the American Revolution called?

Part D — 加拿大歷史 ②（8 題）

  1. 1812 戰爭中被稱為「Saviour of Upper Canada」的將領是誰？Who was called the Saviour of Upper Canada?
  2. 步行 30 公里向英軍報信的加拿大女英雄是誰？Which Canadian woman walked 30 kilometres to warn the British?
  3. 1837 年上加拿大叛亂的領導人是誰？Who led the rebellion in Upper Canada in 1837?
  4. 加拿大邦聯是哪一年、哪一天？On what date was Confederation?
  5. 1867 年建國的四個省是哪四個？Which four provinces formed Canada in 1867?
  6. 加拿大首任總理是誰？Who was the first prime minister?
  7. 「Dominion」一詞來自聖經哪一卷？Which book of the Bible does the word "Dominion" come from?
  8. 國徽箴言「A Mari Usque Ad Mare」是什麼意思？What does A Mari Usque Ad Mare mean?

Part E — 加拿大歷史 ③（8 題）

  1. 加拿大太平洋鐵路哪一年完工？In what year was the Canadian Pacific Railway completed?
  2. 1916 年第一個給婦女省級投票權的省份是哪一省？Which province gave women the vote first, in 1916?
  3. 1921 年加拿大首位女性國會議員是誰？Who became the first woman MP in 1921?
  4. 1917 年哪一場戰役被視為加拿大民族認同的誕生？Which 1917 battle is seen as the birth of the nation?
  5. 加拿大在二戰何時對德宣戰？When did Canada declare war on Germany?
  6. D-Day 登陸時加軍負責哪個灘頭？Which beach did Canadians land on at D-Day?
  7. 1949 年加入成為第十省的是哪一省？Which province joined as the tenth in 1949?
  8. 二戰期間哪一族裔加拿大人被強制遷離 BC？Which Canadians were forced from the BC coast during the war?

Part F — 現代加拿大（7 題）

  1. 加拿大何時加入 UN 與 NATO？When did Canada join the UN and NATO?
  2. Lester B. Pearson 何時獲諾貝爾和平獎？因為什麼貢獻？When did Lester B. Pearson win the Nobel Peace Prize, and for what?
  3. 現行楓葉國旗哪一年採用？In what year was the maple leaf flag adopted?
  4. 1982 年憲法歸國時包含哪一份重要文件？Which document came with patriation in 1982?
  5. 1995 年 Quebec 公投中，留在加拿大的得票率大約是多少？In the 1995 referendum, what share voted to stay in Canada?
  6. 1922 年發現胰島素的兩位加拿大科學家是誰？Which two Canadians discovered insulin in 1922?
  7. Terry Fox 因為什麼成為加拿大英雄？Why is Terry Fox a Canadian hero?
