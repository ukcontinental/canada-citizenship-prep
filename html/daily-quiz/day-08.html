Day 8：政府架構 How Canadians Govern Themselves

對應官方章節：How Canadians Govern Themselves 建議閱讀：PDF p.29–p.32

今日重點

1. 加拿大的政體：3 個關鍵詞（必背）

Canada is a constitutional monarchy, a parliamentary democracy, and a federal state.

  • 君主立憲制：國王是元首，但必須遵守憲法Constitutional monarchy: the King is head of state but is bound by the Constitution
  • 議會民主：人民選出代表組成國會，由國會多數黨組政府Parliamentary democracy: voters elect members of Parliament, and the party with the most seats forms the government
  • 聯邦制：聯邦、省、領地與市政府各有權責Federal state: powers are divided among federal, provincial, territorial and municipal governments

2. 政府的 3 個層級

層級 範圍 例子
Federal 聯邦 全國事務 國防、外交、貨幣、移民、刑法
Provincial 省 / Territorial 領地 各省事務 教育、健保、自然資源、財產法
Municipal 市政 / Local 地方事務 公車、垃圾、警消、地方稅

加拿大有十個省、三個領地，以及數千個市政府。Canada has ten provinces, three territories and thousands of municipalities.

3. 國會 Parliament 的 3 部分（超常考）

The Sovereign, the Senate, and the House of Commons.

a) The Sovereign 君主

  • 現任君主是 King Charles IIIThe Sovereign today is King Charles III
  • 在加拿大由 總督 Governor General 代表He is represented in Canada by the Governor General
  • 現任總督是 Mary Simon，史上首位原住民總督，2021 年上任The Governor General is Mary Simon, the first Aboriginal person to hold the office, appointed in 2021
  • 總督任期大約五年The Governor General usually serves about five years

b) The Senate 參議院

  • 大約 105 位參議員There are about 105 senators
  • 由總理提名、總督任命，不是民選They are appointed by the Governor General on the advice of the Prime Minister, not elected
  • 可任職到 75 歲They may serve until age 75
  • 代表各地區、少數族裔與不同的利益They represent regions, minorities and different interests
  • 常被稱為「冷靜複審之院 Chamber of Sober Second Thought」It is known as the Chamber of Sober Second Thought

c) The House of Commons 眾議院

  • 目前有 338 個席次，席次按人口分配It has 338 seats, distributed by population
  • 由人民直接選出Members are elected directly by the people
  • 任期最長四年A Parliament lasts at most four years
  • 議員稱為 MP，Member of ParliamentA member is called an MP, a Member of Parliament
  • 席次最多的政黨黨魁出任 總理 Prime MinisterThe leader of the party with the most seats becomes Prime Minister

4. 立法流程：一個法案怎麼變法律（必背）

  1. 一讀 First Reading：在眾議院提出法案First reading: the bill is introduced in the House of Commons
  2. 二讀 Second Reading：辯論法案的原則Second reading: the principle of the bill is debated
  3. 委員會審查 Committee Stage：逐條檢視Committee stage: the bill is examined clause by clause
  4. 報告階段 Report Stage：報告修改內容Report stage: the amendments are reported back
  5. 三讀 Third Reading：表決Third reading: the House votes
  6. 送參議院重複同樣程序The Senate repeats the same steps
  7. 御准 Royal Assent：由總督代表君主簽署Royal assent: the Governor General signs on the Sovereign's behalf
  8. 正式成為加拿大的法律The bill becomes the law of Canada

5. 行政分支

  • 總理 Prime Minister 是政府首長，也是多數黨黨魁The Prime Minister is the head of government and leader of the governing party
  • 內閣 Cabinet 由總理任命，成員多半是議員The Cabinet is chosen by the Prime Minister, usually from among MPs
  • 內閣部長 各自負責一個部會Cabinet ministers each run a department
  • 公務員體系 Public Service 負責執行政策The public service carries out the work of government

聯邦 省

8. Ontario 重點（你的省份）

  • 首府是 TorontoThe capital is Toronto
  • 省督 Lieutenant Governor 是 Edith Dumont，2023 年起，是首位出任此職的法裔安省人The Lieutenant Governor is Edith Dumont, since 2023, the first Franco-Ontarian in the post
  • 省長 Premier 是 Doug Ford，進步保守黨，2018 年起，2022 與 2025 連任The Premier is Doug Ford of the Progressive Conservatives, in office since 2018 and re-elected in 2022 and 2025
  • 省議會是 Legislative Assembly of Ontario，議員稱 MPPThe legislature is the Legislative Assembly of Ontario, and members are MPPs
  • 執政黨是安省進步保守黨The governing party is the Progressive Conservative Party of Ontario
  • 官方反對黨黨魁是 Marit Stiles，新民主黨，2023 年起The Leader of the Opposition is Marit Stiles of the NDP, since 2023
