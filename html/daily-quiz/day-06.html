Day 6：現代加拿大 + 1982 憲章

對應官方章節：Modern Canada 建議閱讀：PDF p.25–p.28

今日重點

1. 戰後重建與全球角色

  • 1945 年加拿大是聯合國 UN 的創始國Canada was a founding member of the United Nations in 1945
  • 1949 年加拿大是北約 NATO 的創始國Canada was a founding member of NATO in 1949
  • 1949 年紐芬蘭加入加拿大，成為第十個省Newfoundland joined Canada in 1949 as the tenth province
  • 韓戰 1950 至 1953 年，加拿大派出超過兩萬六千名軍人In the Korean War of 1950 to 1953 Canada sent more than 26,000 troops
  • 加拿大在國際上扮演 中型大國 middle power 的角色，長於調停與維和Canada acts as a middle power, known for mediation and peacekeeping

2. 維和 Peacekeeping

  • Lester B. Pearson 先是外交部長，後來成為總理Lester B. Pearson was foreign minister and later prime minister
  • 1956 年蘇伊士運河危機，他提議由聯合國派出 維和部隊 隔開衝突雙方In the Suez Crisis of 1956 he proposed a United Nations peacekeeping force to separate the sides
  • 1957 年他因此獲得諾貝爾和平獎He won the Nobel Peace Prize for it in 1957
  • 加拿大長期是聯合國維和的重要派兵國Canada has long been a major contributor to UN peacekeeping

3. 1965 國旗

  • 1965 年加拿大採用今天的 紅白楓葉旗In 1965 Canada adopted today's red and white maple leaf flag
  • 在那之前用的是帶英國米字的 Red EnsignBefore that it flew the Red Ensign, which carried the Union Jack

4. 1969 官方語言法

  • 由總理 Pierre Elliott Trudeau 推動Brought in by Prime Minister Pierre Elliott Trudeau
  • 英文與法文在聯邦層級地位平等English and French have equal status at the federal level

5. 1971 多元文化主義

  • Pierre Elliott Trudeau 宣布多元文化成為官方政策Pierre Elliott Trudeau made multiculturalism official government policy

6. 1982 憲法歸國 + 憲章（最重要）

  • 1982 年加拿大完成憲法歸國 Patriation of the ConstitutionIn 1982 Canada patriated its Constitution
  • 由 女王伊莉莎白二世 簽署It was signed by Queen Elizabeth II
  • 其中包含 《加拿大權利與自由憲章》It contains the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms
  • 從此加拿大可自行修憲，不必再經英國國會From then on Canada could amend its own Constitution without the British Parliament

7. 1995 Quebec 第二次獨立公投

  • 結果是留在加拿大 50.6% 對 49.4%，僅差 1.2 個百分點The result was 50.6% to 49.4% to stay — a margin of just 1.2 points
