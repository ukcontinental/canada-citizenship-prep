Day 1：入籍誓詞 + 公民權利與義務

對應官方章節：The Oath of Citizenship、Rights and Responsibilities of Citizenship 建議閱讀：PDF p.2–p.9

今日重點

1. 入籍誓詞 The Oath of Citizenship（必背！口試會被問）

I swear (or affirm) that I will be faithful and bear true allegiance to His Majesty King Charles the Third, King of Canada, His Heirs and Successors, and that I will faithfully observe the laws of Canada, including the Constitution, which recognizes and affirms the Aboriginal and treaty rights of First Nations, Inuit and Métis peoples, and fulfil my duties as a Canadian citizen.

關鍵字記法：How to remember it:

  • King Charles the Third（不是 Queen Elizabeth；2022 年起換新國王）King Charles the Third — not Queen Elizabeth; Canada has had a new king since 2022
  • 誓詞包括憲法 the ConstitutionThe oath covers the Constitution as well as the laws
  • 承認並確認三類原住民的條約權利：First Nations、Inuit、MétisIt recognizes and affirms the Aboriginal and treaty rights of First Nations, Inuit and Métis peoples

2. 加拿大的法律基礎

  • 來自 英國憲政傳統 ＋ 法國民法 (Quebec) ＋ 英國普通法Canadian law flows from the British constitutional tradition, French civil law (Quebec) and English common law
  • 法治 Rule of Law：政府與人民都受法律約束Rule of Law: governments and citizens alike are bound by the law
  • 1982 年憲法 包含《權利與自由憲章 Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms》The Constitution of 1982 contains the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms

3. 權利的四個來源

  1. Magna Carta 大憲章 (1215)Magna Carta, the Great Charter of Freedoms (1215)
  2. 英國權利請願書 1689 Bill of RightsThe English Bill of Rights of 1689
  3. 習慣法 Habeas Corpus（任意拘禁的人有上法庭的權利）Habeas corpus, from English common law — the right to challenge unlawful detention in court
  4. 1982 年加拿大權利與自由憲章The Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms, 1982

4. 憲章保障的 4 大基本自由 Fundamental Freedoms

  1. 良知與宗教自由Freedom of conscience and religion
  2. 思想、信仰、意見、言論自由（含新聞自由）Freedom of thought, belief, opinion and expression, including freedom of speech and of the press
  3. 和平集會自由Freedom of peaceful assembly
  4. 結社自由Freedom of association

5. 公民的權利 Rights

  • 動議自由 Mobility Rights：在加拿大內居住、工作Mobility rights: to live and work anywhere in Canada
  • 原住民人民權利Aboriginal Peoples' rights
  • 官方語言權利：英文／法文Official language rights: English and French
  • 少數族裔語言教育權Minority language educational rights
  • 多元文化遺產權Multiculturalism, a fundamental characteristic of the Canadian heritage

6. 公民的 6 大責任 Responsibilities（這條超常考！）

  1. 守法Obeying the law
  2. 照顧自己與家庭Taking responsibility for oneself and one's family
  3. 服務陪審團Serving on a jury
  4. 投票Voting in elections
  5. 協助社區Helping others in the community
  6. 保護並享受加拿大遺產與環境Protecting and enjoying our heritage and environment

7. 男女平等

  • 加拿大法律承認 男女法律平等Canadian law recognizes the equality of women and men
  • 配偶虐待、榮譽暴力、強迫婚姻、女性割禮 在加拿大屬於 刑事犯罪Spousal abuse, "honour killings", forced marriage and female genital mutilation are crimes in Canada

今日驗收題（20 題）

請先寫答案在另一張紙，做完再看下方「答案與解析」。Write your answers on a separate sheet first, then open the answers below.

  1. 在加拿大入籍誓詞中，公民效忠的對象是誰？In the Oath of Citizenship, to whom do you pledge your allegiance?
  2. 入籍誓詞特別承認哪 3 類原住民的條約權利？Which three Aboriginal peoples' treaty rights does the oath recognize?
  3. 加拿大的法律系統由哪三個傳統匯流而成？Which three legal traditions come together in Canadian law?
  4. 「Rule of Law」中文是什麼？意義為何？What does "Rule of Law" mean?
  5. 1982 年加拿大憲法中包含什麼重要文件？Which important document is part of the Constitution of 1982?
  6. 列出加拿大《權利與自由憲章》保障的 4 大基本自由。List the four fundamental freedoms protected by the Charter.
  7. 「習慣法 Habeas Corpus」保障人民什麼權利？What right does habeas corpus protect?
  8. 列出加拿大公民的 6 大責任。List the six responsibilities of a Canadian citizen.
  9. 哪一項是「責任」而非「權利」：投票／接受醫療／言論自由？Which one is a responsibility rather than a right: voting, receiving health care, or freedom of speech?
  10. 「服務陪審團」屬於權利還是責任？Is serving on a jury a right or a responsibility?
  11. 加拿大男女是否在法律上平等？Are women and men equal under Canadian law?
  12. 配偶虐待、強迫婚姻在加拿大是民事或刑事問題？Are spousal abuse and forced marriage civil or criminal matters in Canada?
  13. Magna Carta 是哪一年訂立的？In what year was Magna Carta signed?
  14. 公民的官方語言權利是哪兩種語言？Which two official languages do language rights cover?
  15. 「Mobility Rights」指什麼權利？What are mobility rights?
  16. 入籍誓詞中提到的國王是誰？Which king is named in the Oath of Citizenship?
  17. 言論自由包不包含新聞自由？Does freedom of expression include freedom of the press?
  18. 多元文化遺產屬於權利還是責任？Is the multicultural heritage a right or a responsibility?
  19. 公民有沒有「不工作的權利」？Is there a "right not to work" in the Charter?
  20. 投票屬於公民責任，那同樣居住在加拿大的「永久居民」可以投票嗎？Voting is a citizen's responsibility — may a permanent resident vote?
答案與解析（點擊展開）
  1. King Charles the Third（查理三世）及其繼承人。2022 年 9 月伊莉莎白二世過世，誓詞已更新為 King Charles the Third。His Majesty King Charles the Third, King of Canada, His Heirs and Successors. The wording changed after Queen Elizabeth II died in September 2022.
  2. First Nations（第一民族）、Inuit（因紐特）、Métis（梅蒂斯）。First Nations, Inuit and Métis peoples.
  3. 英國憲政傳統 ＋ 英國普通法 ＋ 法國民法（主要適用於 Quebec）。The British constitutional tradition, English common law, and French civil law — which is used in Quebec.
  4. 法治。意即沒有人可以凌駕於法律之上，政府也受法律約束。The Rule of Law. It means no person is above the law; governments are bound by it too.
  5. 《加拿大權利與自由憲章》。The Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms.
  6. 良知與宗教自由；思想、信仰、意見、言論（含新聞）自由；和平集會自由；結社自由。Freedom of conscience and religion; freedom of thought, belief, opinion and expression including the press; freedom of peaceful assembly; freedom of association.
  7. 被拘留的人可以請求出庭、挑戰拘留是否合法（防止任意拘禁）。The right to challenge unlawful detention by the state before a court.
  8. 守法；照顧自己與家庭；服務陪審團；投票；協助社區；保護並享受加拿大遺產與環境。Obeying the law; taking responsibility for oneself and one's family; serving on a jury; voting in elections; helping others in the community; protecting and enjoying our heritage and environment.
  9. 投票 是責任（同時也是權利）；言論自由是權利；接受醫療不在憲章層級。Voting is a responsibility as well as a right; freedom of speech is a right; health care is not a Charter freedom.
  10. 責任——而且只有公民才有這項義務。A responsibility, and one that only citizens carry.
  11. 是，法律上完全平等。Yes — women and men are equal under the law.
  12. 刑事犯罪。They are criminal offences.
  13. 1215 年。1215.
  14. English 和 French。English and French.
  15. 在加拿大境內任何地方居住、工作，以及出入境的權利。The right to live and work anywhere in Canada, and to enter and leave the country.
  16. King Charles the Third（查理三世）。King Charles the Third.
  17. 包含。Yes, it does.
  18. 權利。A right.
  19. 沒有這項憲法保障——但也沒有人可以強迫你做特定工作。No, the Charter contains no such right — but no one can force you into a particular job either.
  20. 不可以。投票權只給公民，永久居民還不行。No. Only citizens may vote; permanent residents may not.
