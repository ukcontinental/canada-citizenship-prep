Day 4：加拿大史 ②（1812 戰爭 → 邦聯 1867）

對應官方章節：Canada’s History（中段） 建議閱讀：PDF p.17–p.20

今日重點

1. 1812 年戰爭 War of 1812（必背！）

  • 加拿大對美國Canada against the United States
  • 美國想併吞英屬北美，入侵失敗The United States tried to conquer British North America and failed
  • Major-General Sir Isaac Brock 在 Queenston Heights 陣亡，被譽為「上加拿大的救星」Major-General Sir Isaac Brock was killed at Queenston Heights and is remembered as the Saviour of Upper Canada
  • Chief Tecumseh 是原住民領袖，與英軍同盟對抗美國Chief Tecumseh, a Shawnee leader, allied with the British against the Americans
  • Charles de Salaberry 在魁北克擊退美軍Charles de Salaberry turned back an American attack in Quebec
  • Laura Secord 步行 30 公里向英軍報信，是加拿大歷史最著名的女英雄Laura Secord walked 30 kilometres to warn the British, and is Canada's best-known heroine
  • 1813 年美軍焚毀 York，也就是今天的 TorontoIn 1813 American forces burned York, today's Toronto
  • 報復之下，1814 年英軍焚燒了華盛頓的白宮In return, in 1814 British troops burned the White House in Washington
  • 結果加拿大保住領土，今天的加美邊界由此確立Canada held its territory, and today's Canada-United States border dates from this war

2. 廢除奴隸制度

  • 1793 年 上加拿大由 Lieutenant Governor John Graves Simcoe 率先限制奴隸制In 1793 Lieutenant Governor John Graves Simcoe made Upper Canada the first province to move against slavery
  • 1834 年 大英帝國全面廢除奴隸制度，加拿大也在其中In 1834 slavery was abolished throughout the British Empire, Canada included
  • 地下鐵路 Underground Railroad 協助美國黑奴逃到加拿大獲得自由The Underground Railroad helped enslaved people escape from the United States to freedom in Canada

3. 1837–38 叛亂 The Rebellions

  • 在 上加拿大（今 Ontario） 與 下加拿大（今 Quebec） 同時發生They broke out in both Upper Canada, today's Ontario, and Lower Canada, today's Quebec
  • 領導人是 William Lyon Mackenzie 與 Louis-Joseph PapineauThe leaders were William Lyon Mackenzie and Louis-Joseph Papineau
  • 他們要求 責任政府，也就是內閣要對民選議會負責They demanded responsible government, meaning a cabinet answerable to the elected assembly
  • 叛亂失敗，卻促成了改革The rebellions failed, but they led to reform

4. 責任政府的誕生

  • 1840 年《統一法案》Act of Union 把上、下加拿大合併為「加拿大省」The Act of Union of 1840 joined Upper and Lower Canada into the Province of Canada
  • 1848 至 1849 年，Nova Scotia 與加拿大省出現加拿大第一個責任政府In 1848 and 1849 Nova Scotia and the Province of Canada won the first responsible government in Canada
  • 關鍵人物是 Robert Baldwin 與 Louis-Hippolyte LaFontaine 這對英法搭檔The key figures were Robert Baldwin and Louis-Hippolyte LaFontaine, an English-French partnership
  • Sir Louis-Hippolyte LaFontaine 是第一位由民選多數產生的政府首長Sir Louis-Hippolyte LaFontaine became the first head of a government chosen by an elected majority

5. 邦聯 Confederation 1867（最重要日期）

  • 1867 年 7 月 1 日《英屬北美法案 British North America Act》生效，加拿大誕生On July 1, 1867 the British North America Act took effect and Canada was born
  • 四個建國省份依序是 Ontario、Quebec、Nova Scotia、New BrunswickThe four founding provinces were Ontario, Quebec, Nova Scotia and New Brunswick
  • 首任總理是 Sir John A. Macdonald，$10 鈔票上的人The first prime minister was Sir John A. Macdonald, who appears on the ten-dollar bill
  • 他生於蘇格蘭，被稱為 加拿大國父Born in Scotland, he is known as a Father of Confederation
  • 國號是 Dominion of Canada 加拿大自治領The country was called the Dominion of Canada
  • 名字取自詩篇 72 篇 8 節，也成了國徽箴言 A Mari Usque Ad Mare，從海到海The name comes from Psalm 72:8 and gave Canada its motto, A Mari Usque Ad Mare — from sea to sea

6. 後續省份加入年份（記主要的）

1871 British Columbia

7. 重要早期領袖

  • Sir George-Étienne Cartier：法裔的邦聯之父，Macdonald 的搭檔Sir George-Étienne Cartier, the French-Canadian Father of Confederation and Macdonald's partner
  • Sir Étienne-Paschal Taché：早期的領袖之一Sir Étienne-Paschal Taché, another early leader
  • Sir Leonard Tilley（New Brunswick）：推動邦聯Sir Leonard Tilley of New Brunswick, who pushed for Confederation
  • Sir Charles Tupper（Nova Scotia）：推動邦聯Sir Charles Tupper of Nova Scotia, who did the same
  • Sir Wilfrid Laurier（1896–1911）：首位法裔總理，$5 鈔票上的人Sir Wilfrid Laurier, the first French-Canadian prime minister, on the five-dollar bill
