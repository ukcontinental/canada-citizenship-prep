Day 2：我們是誰（人口、語言、原住民、多元文化）

對應官方章節：Who We Are 建議閱讀：PDF p.10–p.13

今日重點

1. 三大開國民族 Founding Peoples

加拿大由 三大民族 three founding peoples 共同建立：Canada was founded by three founding peoples:

  1. Aboriginal（原住民）Aboriginal peoples
  2. French（法裔）The French
  3. British（英裔）The British

2. 原住民 3 大類別（必背）

中文 英文 占原住民人口 居住地
第一民族 First Nations 約 65% 全國，常住保留區 reserves
因紐特 Inuit 約 4% 北極區（Arctic）
梅蒂斯 Métis 約 30% 草原省 (Prairies)，混血歐裔/原住民

歷史關鍵：Key moments:

  • 加拿大政府 1867 年起 與 First Nations 簽 條約 TreatiesFrom 1867 onward the government signed treaties with First Nations
  • 1800 年代到 1980 年代政府推行 寄宿學校 Residential Schools，造成文化斷裂From the 1800s to the 1980s the government ran residential schools, which cut children off from their culture
  • 2008 年 總理 Stephen Harper 代表政府 正式道歉In 2008 Prime Minister Stephen Harper made a formal apology on behalf of the government

3. 英裔與法裔

  • 法裔 francophones：約 800 萬人，多數在 QuebecFrancophones: about 8 million people, most of them in Quebec
  • 英裔 anglophones：以英文為母語者，約 1,800 萬人Anglophones: about 18 million people whose first language is English
  • Quebec 有八成講法語，是 北美最大的法語社區About 80% of Quebecers speak French — the largest French-speaking community in North America
  • Acadians：17 世紀起在大西洋省份的法裔居民Acadians are the descendants of French colonists who settled in the Atlantic provinces from the 1600s
  • Quebecers：Quebec 的居民Quebecers are the people who live in Quebec

4. 加拿大的雙語

  • 英文與法文同為官方語言English and French are the two official languages
  • 1969 年《官方語言法》Official Languages Act：保障兩種語言平等地位The Official Languages Act of 1969 gives the two languages equal status
  • 多數法裔住在 Quebec、新布藍瑞克北部、安大略東部與北部、曼尼托巴Most francophones live in Quebec, northern New Brunswick, eastern and northern Ontario, and Manitoba

5. 多元文化 Multiculturalism

  • 加拿大是 世界上最多元的國家之一Canada is one of the most diverse countries in the world
  • 大多數加拿大人 是移民或移民的後代Most Canadians are immigrants or the descendants of immigrants
  • 早期主要移民來自英國、愛爾蘭、法國The earliest immigrants came mainly from Britain, Ireland and France
  • 現在最大的移民來源是 亞洲（中國、印度、菲律賓）Today the largest source of newcomers is Asia — China, India and the Philippines
  • 1971 年由總理 Pierre Elliott Trudeau 推出多元文化主義，成為官方政策In 1971 Prime Minister Pierre Elliott Trudeau made multiculturalism official government policy

6. 主要宗教

  • 基督宗教是最大的宗教，其中天主教 Catholic 人數最多Christianity is the largest religion, with Catholics the biggest group
  • 其他還有伊斯蘭教、印度教、錫克教、佛教、猶太教Others include Islam, Hinduism, Sikhism, Buddhism and Judaism
  • 加拿大保障宗教自由Canada guarantees freedom of religion

7. 人口與大城市

  • 全國人口約 3,800 萬到 4,000 萬The population is about 38 to 40 million
  • 約八成的人住在城市About 80% of Canadians live in cities
  • 三大都會：Toronto 多倫多、Montreal 蒙特婁、Vancouver 溫哥華The three largest cities are Toronto, Montreal and Vancouver

今日驗收題（20 題）

  1. 加拿大的三大開國民族是哪三類？Who are the three founding peoples of Canada?
  2. 加拿大原住民分為哪三個主要群體？What are the three main groups of Aboriginal peoples?
  3. 哪一群原住民住在北極區？Which Aboriginal group lives in the Arctic?
  4. 哪一群原住民是歐裔與原住民的混血後代？Which group descends from both Aboriginal and European ancestors?
  5. 「Residential Schools」是什麼？造成什麼影響？What were the residential schools, and what was their effect?
  6. 加拿大政府在哪一年為寄宿學校正式道歉？由哪位總理代表？In what year did the government apologize for the residential schools, and which prime minister did it?
  7. 加拿大的兩種官方語言是什麼？What are Canada's two official languages?
  8. 哪一年通過《官方語言法》？In what year was the Official Languages Act passed?
  9. Quebec 的官方語言是什麼？What is the official language of Quebec?
  10. 「Acadians」是指什麼人？Who are the Acadians?
  11. 「Quebecers」是指什麼人？Who are Quebecers?
  12. 多元文化在哪一年成為官方政策？由哪位總理推動？In what year did multiculturalism become official policy, and under which prime minister?
  13. 加拿大最大的宗教傳統是什麼？What is the largest religious tradition in Canada?
  14. 加拿大人口大約多少？Roughly what is Canada's population?
  15. 加拿大三大都會是哪三個？What are Canada's three largest cities?
  16. 法裔人口大約多少？About how many francophones are there?
  17. 大多數加拿大人住在城市還是鄉村？Do most Canadians live in cities or in the countryside?
  18. 「First Nations」中文叫什麼？What does "First Nations" mean?
  19. 政府最早從哪一年開始與原住民簽訂條約？From what year did the government begin signing treaties with Aboriginal peoples?
  20. 在加拿大，所有人是否都必須信同一個宗教？Must everyone in Canada follow the same religion?
答案與解析（點擊展開）
  1. Aboriginal（原住民）、French（法裔）、British（英裔）。Aboriginal peoples, the French and the British.
  2. First Nations、Inuit、Métis。First Nations, Inuit and Métis.
  3. Inuit（因紐特）。The Inuit.
  4. Métis（梅蒂斯），主要住在草原省份。The Métis, who live mostly in the Prairie provinces.
  5. 1800 至 1980 年代政府強制原住民兒童入住的學校，禁止使用母語與傳統文化，造成嚴重創傷。Government-run schools from the 1800s to the 1980s that Aboriginal children were forced to attend; they were forbidden to speak their languages or practise their culture, and the harm was lasting.
  6. 2008 年，由總理 Stephen Harper 代表政府道歉。In 2008, by Prime Minister Stephen Harper on behalf of the government.
  7. English 和 French。English and French.
  8. 1969 年。1969.
  9. 法文 French——Quebec 是唯一以法文為唯一官方語言的省。French. Quebec is the only province whose sole official language is French.
  10. 17 世紀起在大西洋省份定居的法裔居民後代。The descendants of French colonists who settled in the Atlantic provinces from the 1600s.
  11. 居住在 Quebec 的人。The people who live in Quebec.
  12. 1971 年，由 Pierre Elliott Trudeau 推動。1971, under Pierre Elliott Trudeau.
  13. 基督宗教，其中以天主教人數最多。Christianity, with Catholics the largest group.
  14. 約 3,800 萬到 4,000 萬人。About 38 to 40 million.
  15. Toronto、Montreal、Vancouver。Toronto, Montreal and Vancouver.
  16. 約 800 萬人。About 8 million.
  17. 城市，約八成。In cities — about 80%.
  18. 第一民族。The first peoples of Canada, the largest of the three Aboriginal groups.
  19. 1867 年起，也就是邦聯之後。From 1867, that is, after Confederation.
  20. 不必。加拿大保障宗教自由。No. Canada guarantees freedom of religion.
